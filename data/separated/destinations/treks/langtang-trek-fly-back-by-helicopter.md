The Langtang Valley is located only 90 km north of Kathmandu. So Langtang Trek Fly Back by Helicopter is the best choice for visitors to Nepal. Langtang is close to the border of Tibet (China), the valley is dominated by Langtang Lirung (7246m), the highest peak in the area. This is a superb introduction to trekking in Nepal among some of the most beautiful scenery in the whole country and fascinating unspoiled Sherpa villages. We follow the Trisuli River north out of Kathmandu, and trek through Trisuli Bazaar, Dhunche then we get to Syabrubensi, a Sherpa village located at the very edge of the Langtang National Park.
We then swing east into the Langtang Valley, crossing the river several times. As we travel up along a George-like lower section of the valley. We pass through oak and rhododendron forests. We will even cross a glacial moraine on our way to the Tamang village of Langtang. After this beautiful Langtang village trek. We will fly back by helicopter from Kyanjing Gompa to Kathmandu with its beautiful Mountain views. We hope this Helicopter Trek to Langtang will be more worthful and incredible for your lifetime.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Drive from kathmandu to Shyaphru Basi (1460m)
Today will be a long day drive. We will be having drive mostly in the Hilly Region of Nepal. This Drive can provide you detail landscape of Hilly region.
We will head as towards Syabrubesi from Kathmandu as we finish our breakfast. It will be 6-7 hours of Trek.
We will reach Syabrubesi by evening and you can explore around the small town.
Overnight at Syabrubesi
- Day 03: Trek From Shyaphru Besi to Lama Hotel (2470m)
We will have our breakfast at around 7 am and will be leaving towards Lama Hotel. Syabrubesi is also the separating point to different destination of Langtang Region.
Today our Trek will be 6-7 Hours long. The trails are narrow and our trail upto Lama Hotel will be with river bank and gorge of two big ridge.
Overnight at Lama hotel
- Day 04: Trek From Lama Hotel To Langtang (3430m)
Today as we finish our breakfast we will head towards Langtang Village. Langang Village is a small village between two Ridge. You will find a small river flowing from the gorge.
The trails are narrow and slope. you will encounter the domestic animals on the way with small shrubs throughout.
Overnight at Langtang Village
- Day 05: Trek From Langtang to Kyanjin Gomba (3870mm)
Today our destination will be Kyanjin Gompa. We will head to a beautiful village which is below one of the best view point of the trail.
Kyangin Gompa is the final destination of Langtang Valley Trek.
Today we will explore Kyanjin Gompa and prepare for the short climb of Tserko Ryi.
Overnight at Kyanjin Gompa
- Day 06: Day Hiking In Kyanjin Gomba
Today we will hike early in the morning to Tserko Ri which has an elevation of 5033m. from the view point you can see many mountains. Mountains such as Tsangbu Ri, Langtang Lirung, Baden Powell peaks and many small peaks can be seen. You will also see the beautiful gorge of Langtang Village, small glacier lake as well as villages of this Region.
We will enjoy and capture our best memories at the top of the peak. After we stay there for while we will descend back to Kyanjin Gompa.
Overnight at Kyanjin Gompa
- Day 07: Fly back by Helicopter to Kathmandu
After the morning exploration, we will be picked by helicopter. We will experience the Mountain Flight and and reach back to Kathmandu for Lunch.
We can freshen up yourself after this beautiful short Trek in Langtang Region.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 08: Free Day In Kathmandu
Today will be a freeday. You can explore around and make your shopping if needed.
Today you can explore the UNESCO Heritage sites around which are within very short range.
UNESCO Sites nearby:
- Swyambhunath Stup
- Boudhanath Stupa
- Kathmandu Durbar square
- Pashupatinath Temple
- Patan Durbar Square
- Bhaktapur Durbar Square
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 09: Final departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Langtang Valley Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
