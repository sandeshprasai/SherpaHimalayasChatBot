The Annapurna Circuit Trek with Tilicho Lake takes you through the lowland villages occupied by Brahmin and Chhetri ethnic people to far-off settlements of Manang.
Descend into the Kali Gandaki Gorge, the deepest gorge on the planet. And also visit the mesmerizing sapphire lake of Tilicho- the highest located lake on Earth. Walk on the beautiful trail that leads to the Thorong La Pass. Which is also one of the highest passes present in the mountain.
Encounter a wide range of culturally rich and ethnically diverse villages and settlements. As you walk on beautiful trekking trails through the very heart of the Annapurna region. Experience the differences on the terrain that ranges from subtropical woodlands to dry arid landscapes as you move higher.
The Annapurna Circuit Trek with Tilicho Lake also lets you visit the spiritual hub of the region- Muktinath and look at sights of amazing mountain peaks of Annapurna from one of the most famous vantage points in the country. Situated at an altitude of 4,970 meters, the Tilicho Lake is the highlight of the trek.
Famous as one of the most winsome destinations for trekking in the country, the Tilicho Lake has a distinct emerald glow in its waters. After the construction of a road up to Manang, the Annapurna Circuit Trek with Tilicho Lake presents one of the most alluring trekking journeys in the entire region.
Drive to the village of Chame from Kathmandu and the trek to the Thorong La pass via Manang. Hike up to the cerulean waters of the Tilicho Lake for a sight that is sure to forever be engraved into your minds. Experiencing the best elements of the region, the Annapurna Circuit with Tilicho Lake Trek is sure to leave you with beguiling natural presentations of the region’s wonders.
Cross the Thorong La Pass and descend down to Muktinath. Sacred to both the Hindu and the Buddhist people, Muktinath is located at an altitude of 3,710 meters at the foot of the Mountain Pass. Known as the place of liberation, Muktinath adds a spiritual sense to the journey.
Trek to Jomsom and then fly back to Pokhara. The trekking trails are cover by lush vegetations and sights of the distant mountain peaks augment the feel of surrealism to the trekking journey.
The whole experience of the Annapurna Circuit Trek with Tilicho Lake is unforgettable and brimming with the natural, cultural and Himalayan elegance of one of the most exquisite regions in the country.
You can see views of the Annapurna range, the Dhaulagiri range and the Manaslu Himalayas as you walk on paths that reach the far northern peaks all the way to the Tibetan plateaus.

Accompanied by professionals, you would be subjected to hiking to the cyan Tilicho Lake and look at the mesmerizing wonder of nature in all its glory.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival day in Kathmandu (1300m)
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trek Preparation in Kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Tilicho Lake with Thorongla Pass Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you to use company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplace in airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 03: Drive from Kathmandu to Besi Sahar and local jeep to Dhara Pani (1960m)
Today we will have a long day drive. As we finish our breakfast we will head towards Dharapani via Besisahar. It will take 6 Hours to drive to besisahar by private vehicle. After short rest we will again have to drive 2 hours long offroad to reah afternoon camp Dharapani.
This drive will be very interesting and perfect time to know more about the landscape of Nepal.
Overnight at Dharapani
- Day 04: Trek from Dharapani to Chame (2,710m/8,891feet), 5 - 6 hours
Today is our first Trek day. We will start our Trek from Dharapani, heading towards Chame which is elevated at altitude of 2710m.
Your trek will be 5-6 hours long along the river side enjoying beautiful nature and mountain such as Mt. Lamjung and Mt. Annapurna
Overnight at Chame
- Day 05: Trek from Chame to Pisang (3,300m/10824feet), 5 - 6 hours
After we finish our breakfast we will head towards Pisang. The trek will be normal with slight slopes. It will take Normally 5 hours to reach Pisang.
You will find two Pisang; Upper Pisang and Lower Pisang. The camp site will be decided by then while we get close to. Both the Pisang are nearby so, it is similar wherever we stay for camp. You can witness Mt. Annapurna II directly from Pisang
Overnight at Pisang
- Day 06: Trek from Pisang to Ngawal (3700m/12139 ft) 4-5 hours
Today you will have a normal trek with an almost 2-hour uphill climb. As you complete the uphill climb you will be introduced to one of the oldest and traditional villages of the Annapurna Region; well known as Gyaru.
After a short break at Gyaru with a furthermore, 1 and half hour normal trek you will reach Ngawal. One of the best locations providing you breathtaking views of Mt. Annapurna II, IV, II, Mt. Gangapurna, Mt. Tilicho, Mt. Chulu East, Far East, and Mt. Pisang.
Overnight at Ngawal
- Day 07: Trek from Ngawal to Manang (3,500m/11,482feet), 3-4 hours
Today we will reach a beautiful small village called Manang. Manang is also the perfect location to acclimatize your body to altitude. Today you will have a normal descend and sideways trek.
With normal 3-4 hours of Trek, we will reach Manang.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 08: Acclimatazation Day In Manang
Today is the acclimatization day. This day is specially kept to let your body acclimatize with the altitude and the environment. You can take this day as a rest day too.
This day is also known as hike up sleep low. Today we will hike to Chongur View Point crossing through Gangapurna Glacier. you will also encounter beautiful glacier lake.
After we finish our exploration around we will come back to camp and be prepared for the next day.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 09: Trek From Manang to Shree Kharka (3,800m)
Manang is also a location which separate two trails; one to Tilicho lake and another towards Thorongla Pass directly.
Since we will visit the Tilicho lake in the beginning we will head towards Shree Kharka. Today we will need 5-6 hours of trek.
Overnight at Shree Kharka
- Day 10: Trek from Shree Kharka to Tilicho Basecamp (4140m)
As we finish our breakfast we will be heading towards Tilicho Base Camp. Today our trek will be a normal 4-5 hours long. The trails are narrow and slight slope.
- Day 11: Trek from Tilicho Base camp to Tilicho Lake (4949m)Visit and back to Tilicho Base camp
Today is the day to reach our first destination; Tilicho Lake. We will head early morning to Tilicho lake.
We will explore Tilicho lake take beautiful pictures of the lake and come back to base camp.
- Day 12: Trek from Sri Kharka to Yak kharka (4220m)
Now our next destination will be Thorongla Pass. For the day we will trek upto Yak Kharka. We will pass over the ridge. You will need 6-7 hours of Trek.
Overnight at Yak Kharka
- Day 13: Trek from Yak kharka to Thorang High camp (4800m)
Today your Trek will be more longer then the past day. We are now closed to Thorongla Pass. Our today’s camp will be below the pass; which is also known as thorongla pass.
It will normally Take 6-7 hours , but the trail is not much hard. We will hike normally with gentle slope and with normal uphills.
Overnight at Thornag High Camp
- Day 14: Trek from Thorang High Camp to Thorang-la pass via Muktinath (3800m)
Today is the day to reach upto your final & main destination of this entire Trek. Its dream of millions but only few take this encouraging trek and make their life filled with beautiful memories and adventure.
We will trek as we finish our breakfast. We will trek uphill until we reach Thorongla Pass (5416m). The trail is not much difficult.
After continuous 3-4 hours climb of uphill slopes we will reach at Thorongla Pass. which is also the main attraction of Annapurna Circuit Trek. We will enjoy our successful Trek there and take some beautiful pictures capturing the memories.
This point can let you view two big and famous trekking regions of Nepal. Annapurna Region and Mustang region.
Now after the pass we will enter into mustang region. Today our stay will be at Muktinath (3800m). It will take normally 2 hours downhill.
Overnight at Muktinath
- Day 15: Trek from Muktinath to Jomsom (2700m)
Now we will head for the another beautiful attraction Ghorepani Poonhill. Today our camp will be Jomsom. We will either trek or drive to Jomsom.
It will normally take 5-6 hours by trek and 1 hour by drive. If you decide to trek down then you will visit one of the most historic village in mustang Village known as Kagbeni.
You will witness small mud houses and old temples with beautiful tibetan lifestlye. your trek will be a worth decision going through Kagbeni.
Overnight at Jomsom
- Day 16: Jomsom to Pokhara by flight
As we finish our Breakfast today we will have direct flight from Jomsom to Pokhara.
Our flight will be early in the morning so, if you can freshen yourself as you reach pokhara and explore around.
- Day 17: Trek from Pokhara to Kathamandu by flight
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Tilicho Lake with Thorongla Pass Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free time to explore and shop for your family or else you can also make short city tour.
- Day 18: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Tilicho lake with Thorongla Pass Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
