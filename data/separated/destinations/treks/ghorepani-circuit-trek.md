Shortest Annapurna Base Camp Trek – 10 Days, Annapurna base camp trek is an amazing walk...
Ghorepani Circuit trek route is one of the most visited areas of the Annapurna trail because of the easy climb up and most varied landscape features of the entire region. Probably Landhruk trek is suitable for every level of trekkers-beginners, children with a family holiday, and old age groups, and due to its well-managed local eco teahouse lodges facilities for food and accommodation along the way. This trekking is also suitable for those who have limited time but dream of a glimpse of snow-capped mountain peaks and still want a real Himalayan cultural experience in a lifetime. In the spring season, there are 9 different species of wild rhododendron flowers bloom which adds this trekking trail to turn into one of the real Shangri La on Earth.
Ghorepani, Chomrung, and Landhruk trekking can be done throughout the year except for monsoon season mid-June to mid-September and its easy trail begins further of Nayapul (1070m) trailhead up to Ghorepani (2800m). Along with this Ghorepani Circuit Trek, you will be enjoying the fascinating tradition of local Gurung villages and their typical cultural reflection passing through rhododendron, magnolia, and oak forest together with breathtaking views of Mt. Fishtail (6993m), Annapurna South (7219m), Himchuli (6441m) and amazing mountain panorama views from Poon Hill (3210m) including entire Dhaulagiri (8167m) massif and Annapurna range series dominating the skyline beyond with Annapurna I (8091m) peak. Throughout the trek, you will pass the various villages like; Chomrung, Landruk, Dhampus, etc. which offer magnificent views of Annapurnas from low altitude & it is best for family, elder people, and children.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival In Kathmandu
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Kathmnadu to Pokhara
Today we will either drive or take a flight to Pokhara. For flight will will take around 30-40 minutes depending upon the weather. If you take the option to travel by Vehicle then it will take around 8-9 hours normally. The time will take more if you get into traffic.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 03: Pokhara to Hile and Trek to Ulleri
Today will be the first day of Trek. As we finish our breakfast we will have a short drive to Hile. It will take around one and half hour of Drive.
As we reach Hile we will start our Trek towards Ulleri. It will take 4-5 hours of Trek to reach Ulleri.
Overnight at Ulleri
- Day 04: Trek from Ulleri to Ghorepani (2,850 m/ 9,350 feet), 4 - 5 hours trek
Today as we finish our breakfast we will head towards Ghorepani. With normal uphill Trek of 4-5 hours through the rhododendron forest enjoying small village you will reach Ghorepani in the afternoon.
This is also the last point or final destintion of Ghorepani Poonhill Trek. This point provides you beautiful himlayan range of Annapurna Range mountains.
Overnight at Ghorepani
- Day 05: Trek from Ghorepani to Tadapani
Today early in the morning we will make a short hike to Poonhill 3200m to enjoy the beautiful morning sunrise view over the Annapurna Range himalayas.
After enjoying the beautiful time we will go back to Ghorepani and have our breakfast head towards Tadapani.
The trail is mostly downhill through normal forest.
Overnight at Tadapani
- Day 06: Trek from Tadapani to Chhomrung
As we finish our breakfast we will head towards Chhomrong. We will descend downward to the river bank and cross the small village.
After crossing the river with normal uphill climb we will reach chhomrong.
Chomrong is a beautiful village at a hill point from were you will have sight of amazing landscape and Mountains. Beautiful view of Mt. Machhapurche can be witness.
Overnight at Chhomrong
- Day 07: Trek from Chhomrong to Landhruk
We will start today trek as usual after having breakfast. Today our Destination will be Landhruk. Today we will descend down to Jhinu and then come down along with the river called Modi Khola.
After continuous walk along the river we will head slight left and climb a normal uphill to reach the small town Landruk.
Landruk can be a perfect location for a sample of Terrace farming in Hilly Region
Overnight at Landhruk
- Day 08: Trek from Landhruk to Dampus
Today our final destination will be Dhampus. We will have normal slight uphill towards right. After few hours of walk we will reach a place called Pothana.
After a hour of Trek we will reach the beautiful and cultural village Dhampus. You will find small hotels which are built on the hill.
Overnight at Dhampus
- Day 09: Dampus to Pokhara
Today after having our breakfast we will trek down for a hour and reach a place called Phedi.
As we reach Phedi we will drive to Pokhara by local Jeep available at that spot.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 10: Pokhara to kathmandu
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Ghorepani Circuit Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free time as a day to explore and shop for your family or else you can also make short city tour.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 11: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Ghorepani Circuit Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
