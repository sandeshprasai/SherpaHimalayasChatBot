Phoksundo Lake Trek lies in Dolpo, which is also the largest territorial district in Nepal. It is certainly one of the most beautiful and splendid place. The exotic sites and natural sceneries lures people all over the world , thus making it a must visit destination. Dolpo has been captured beautifully on film by the renowned director Eric Valli in his movie “Caravan”.
Phoksundo Lake Trek is known as one of the most scenic and beautiful natural tourist destination in Nepal. Mirroring the snowy Kanjiroba massif , the turquoise lake waters look heavenly.
No wonder this lake is regarded sacred by Buddhists and followers of Bon-po religion.
The Phoksundo Teahouse Trek is Dolpo’s one and only teahouse trek. This trek is fairly easy as no high passes involves. The trek starts from Juphal, where the only airstrip in Dolpo is located.
The trail passes through wonderful Himalayan forests and picturesque traditional villages. The rivers and waterfalls on the trail are some of Nepal’s most magnificent.
In addition to natural beauty, the trekking area also is rich in wildlife. One may get to see some endangered species like the snow leopard, musk deer, blue sheep, great Tibetan sheep, Himalayan tahr etc. Within the Shey Phoksundo National Park lies our destination Phoksundo Lake.

Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Pre paration day in Kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Shey Phoksundo Lake Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Domestic Flight from Kathmandu to Nepalgunj
Today as you finish your breakfast you will be picked by your guide in the hotel and head towards TIA airport and make a scenic flight to Nepalgunj.
Overnight at Nepalgunj
- Day 04: Flight form Nepalgunj to Juphal/ (2475m) and Trek from Juphal to Dunai (2145m)/ 3 hrs
Today again we will head to Nepalgunj airport after we finish our breakfast. We will have an amazing flight to Juphal.
You will be witness beautiful monasteries, river and landscape from your flight. after around 35 minutes of flight, you will be at Juphal. After a short trek, you will reach a small and beautiful town Dunai.
Overnight at Dunai
- Day 05: Trek from Dunai to Chhepka (2670m)/ 6-7 hrs
As we finish our breakfast we will slowly head to the trail of Dolpa. Our trail; will be across a small village called Sulighar. We will have an average of 5 hours of Trek today. The trail is a beautiful and comfortable path.
After finishing all the documentation we will enter into She Phoksundo National Park.
Now we trek into isolated small villages full of green vegetation and also a suspension bridge. This Trek is one of the perfect treks to exchange culture and tradition.
Overnight at Chhepka
- Day 06: Chhepka to Jharana Hotel/ 5-6 hrs
As we finish breakfast, we will head towards our destination Jharana Hotel. Jarana means Waterfall. The trail is similar to the previous day. Today we will inter into Lower Dolpo Area.
Today we will come across the highest waterfall in Nepal which is 330m. You can also have a far sight of Phoksundo lake from here.
overnight at Jarana Hotel
- Day 07: Trek from Jharana Hotel to Phoksundo Lake
As we finish our breakfast we will head towards our final destination which can be compared with paradise place. As said, ‘the best view comes after the hardest climb.’ we will move ahead with steep descend and narrow path. After a continuous walk for 4-5 hours, you will arrive at FShey Phoksundoi Lake.
Overnight at She Phoksundo Lake.
- Day 08: Explore around Phoksundo Lake
Today we will explore the lake and also visit a small village known as Ringmo Village. There is also a very old monastery which is known as Tshowa Monastery.
This monastery is at the ridge top above the amazing Phoksundo lake. As we make our short hike to the top we will slowly return back to camp.
Overnight at Phoksundo Lake
- Day 09: Trek from Phoksundo Lake to Chhepka (2520m)/ 6-7 hrs
After a memorable time at Shey Phoksundo, we will trek back to Chekpa from the same trail we climbed. Today will be a long trek back which will be around 6-7 hours.
Overnight at Chhekpa.
- Day 10: Trek from Chhepka to Juphal
As we finish our breakfast, we will start descending back to Juphal. Your trek will be as the previous day 6-7 hours.
Overnight at Juphal
- Day 11: Flight from Juphal to Nepalgunj and then take a connecting flight to Kathmandu
Today we will make our morning flight to Nepalgunj and after a quick meal, we will have an afternoon flight to Kathmandu.
Overnight at Kathmandu.
- Day 12: Free Day in Kathmandu
Today you can Explore the Thamel market and shop for your family. You can also make a short city tour to explore some World Heritage Site.
You will be invited for the Last farewell dinner with Sherpa Himalaya Executive.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 13: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and a certificate for Shey Phoksundo Lake Trek of your completion of with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
