Manaslu Circuit is a challenging trek around Manaslu the world’s eighth highest mountain and crossing Larkya La pass. This trek includes a visit to Tsum Valley. This is one of Buddhism’s sacred hidden valleys called “Beyul” with important ancient monasteries and remote villages.
Tsum Valley Trek was only open for trekkers three years ago and therefore is a new place to explore with little impact from tourism. This trek would appeal to anyone who is looking for a challenging trek in a remote region of the Nepal Himalaya.
The trail passes through a geographically spectacular and culturally fascinating area with scenery similar to the classic Annapurna Circuit although the trails are not as popular with trekking groups.
The trek starts at Soti Khola and passes through bamboo forests and Gurung villages in the narrow Buri Gandaki gorge. After Lho village we enter the Nupri region inhabited by Tibetan immigrants where you will see colorful prayer flags, mani walls, and monasteries along the way.
After Samdo village we start the approach towards the Larkya La pass, crossing this high Himalayan pass is a challenging day although would be a good objective for a fit hill walker with no previous mountaineering experience.
The view from the Larkya La is superb where we will see Himal Chuli, Manaslu, Cheo Himal, and Himlung Himal mountains. Click here for detailed explanation and discussion about how the different Classic Circuit treks in Nepal compare and the pros & cons of each one.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrive to Kathmandu
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Preparing For trekking in Kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Tsum Valley with Manaslu Circuit Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Drive to MachhaKhola (900m)
Today, we will head toward Soti khola by drive. The road up to Dhading Besi is pretty normal but, as you head towards Soti khola you will face off roads with bumpy tracks for 9-10 hours.
Overnight at MachhaKhola
- Day 04: Trek to Jagat (1,440m)
As of the previous day, we will have a similar time of Trek which will be between 6-7 hours. Today our camp elevation will be increased by 400m + elevation.
We will head towards Jagat as we finish our powerful breakfast. Our trail will be along the river and normal forest. Today you will also go by a natural Hotspring named Tatopani.
Overnight at Jagat.
- Day 05: Trek to Lhokpa in Tsum Valley (2,240m)
Today we will Trek to Lhokpa. A small village rich in tradition and old arts, sculptures. Along the trail, the mountain views come and go with every different location.
Our trek starts with an uphill climb up to Salleri and then descends down towards Sirdibas. Today we will come across many small villages mostly inhabit with Gurung people.
We will trek forward leaving still villages behind crossing the gorge and enjoying waterfalls. You will find a thin pine forest and Rhododendron forest. After a few hours of Trek from a village known as Chisopani, we will arrive at today’s destination Lhokpa.
Overnight at Lhokpa
- Day 06: Trek to Dumje (2,460m).
Today our destination will be Dumje. As usual, as we finish our breakfast we will start our Trek.
You will encounter a village known as Lungwa Khola. These villages are nearby the rivers. We will trek through small Pine forest and reach Gumlung village.
With a continuous walk through the small rivers and villages, we will enter into the lower Tsum Valley.
Overnight at Dumje.
- Day 07: Trek from Dumje- Chule ( 3,361 m)
As we finish our breakfast we will head towards Chule. Today you will climb a suspension bridge that will lead towards Rachen Gompa. Rachen Gompa is a monastery which is also known as Nunnery Gompa.
You will encounter many small villages Lar, Shiar Khola, Pangdun Village, and some Buddhist monasteries.
Overnight at Chule
- Day 08: Trek to Mu Gompa (3710m)
Today will be Trek at high elevation nearby the border of Tibet. After a continuous walk of 3-4 hours you will arrive at Mu Gompa.
You will find amazing landscape of both Nepal and Tibet.
Overnight at Mu Gompa.
- Day 09: Acclimatization Day in Mu Gompa
Today is an acclimatization day. this day is kept in order to save you from altitude sickness. We will explore one of the biggest monasteries in this region. Mu Gompa.
You will experience one of the remote are of whole Nepal. Today you can also hike up to Pika Himal Base Camp which has an elevation of 4865m
Overnight at Mu Gompa
- Day 10: Trek to Chumling in Tsum valley (2,386m)
Today as we finish our breakfast we will Trek down to Chumling Village. The trail down is the same we climbed to Mu Gompa.
Overnight ar Chumling
- Day 11: Trek to Deng (1,860m)
As always, as we finish our breakfast we will head towards the days’ Destination. Our destination today is Deng. Today we will return from Tsum Valley and catch the trail of Manaslu Circuit Trek.
Today’s Trail will be combination of both ascend and descend. The trail to Deng is comparatively easier.
Overnight at Deng
- Day 12: Trek to Namrung (2,540m):6-7 Hours Trek
Today our destination will be Gyap. As usual, we will head as we finish our breakfast we will head uphill trail. We will go across the beautiful field of Mustards with flowers everywhere during the right season. You will witness pleasant sounds of waterfalls, very old culture, art, and sculpture in Gyap. As we take rest in Gyap we will furthermore trek for around 4 hours and reach Namrung.
Overnight at Namrung.
- Day 13: Trek to Samagaon (3,450m)
Today we will cross a river known as Hinan river. Hinan is formed by the Linda Glacier. You will have a beautiful view of Naike Peak, Manaslu North(7,774m) and Manaslu (8,150m).
As we head ahead we will reach a small village known as Lho Gaon, from where you will get a breathtaking view of Mt. Manaslu. You will also find a very old monastery Ribung Gompa.
Overnight at Samagaon.
- Day 14: Day walk from Samagaon (3,450m)
Today is an acclimatization day. On this day we will Trek high and sleep low which will help us to prevent from Altitude sickness. We will explore nearby villages and also visit Pungyen gompa and Glacial Lake is known as Birendra Tal. It will take 4-5 hours hike to reach here.
Overnight at Samagaon.
- Day 15: Trek to Samdo (3,860m)
Today we will have a normal trek which will be around 5 hours. Our today’s destination is Samdo.
We will climb uphill leaving Budi Gandaki River behind.
Overnight at Samdo
- Day 16: Trek to Dharamasala (4480m).
Today we will trek upto the Base camp of Larkya. The trail is normal as previous days. We will camp at Dharamshala which is also known as Larkya Phedi. The place has a small hotel which provides basic lodging and food facility.
Overnight at Dharamshala
- Day 17: Trek over Larkya la pass (5,213m) to Bimtang (3,590m)
Today we will make one of the best and adventurous passes of the Manaslu region, Larkya La Pass has an elevation of 5160m.
We will slowly head uphill towards the pass covered with thin snow. You will have an amazing view of many mountains such as Mt. Himlung(7,126 m), Mt. Cheo(6,820 ), Mt.Kangkuru(6,981 m) and Annapurna I (7,937 ).
As we move across the pass we will climb down which is an almost steep trail, across moraines. Our Overnight will be at Bimthang.
Overnight at Bimthang.
- Day 18: Trek to Tilije (2300m).
As we reach Bimthang we will start our Trek back. Today we will trek to Tilije. The trails go easier down and again will encounter more greenery and forest.
Overnight at Tilije.
- Day 19: Trek from Tilije to Dharapani and same day drive to Kathmandu via Besisahar
Today early morning as you finish your breakfast Trek to Dharapani one hours and you will be picked by vehicle which is already kept ready by the Sherpa Himalaya.
You will be traveling back to Kathmandu with a beautiful experience and taking all those beautiful memories captured with you.
You will arrive at Kathmandu in the evening.
Overnight at KTM hotel.
- Day 20: Final Departure
Today morning Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone and a certificate for your completion of Tsum Valley with Manaslu Circuit Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
