Kanchenjunga is third highest mountain in the world, and the most easterly of the 8000 meter peaks of the Himalaya. The name means Five Treasures of the Great Snow, a reference to its five summits.
The name is also spelled Kanchenjunga, but according to the Himalayan Journal article, people who know the Tibetan language strongly insist that the ‘g’ should be there.
The huge massif of Kanchenjunga is buttressed by great ridges running roughly due east to west and north to south, forming a giant ‘X’. These ridges contain a host of spectacular 6-7000 meter peaks.
On the East Ridge in Sikkim, is Siniolchu (22,600′ / 6888m), regarded as one of the most beautiful mountains in the world. The west ridge culminates in the magnificent Jannu (25,294′ / 7710m) with its imposing north face.
To the south, clearly visible form Darjeeling, is Kabru (24,002′ / 7,316 m). The north ridge contains The Twins and Tent Peak, and runs up to the Tibetan border by the Jongsong La, a 20,080′ (6,120 m) pass. You will experience an extreme trekking lesson, far from city’s hustle on the lap of the Himalayas that is for sure!

Day 01: Arrival day in Kathmandu (1300m)
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trek Preparation in Kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Kanchanjunga Circuit Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and the rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Flight Kathmandu to Bhadrapur (300m) and Drive to Ilam
Today you will have a morning flight to Bhadrapur. Bhadrapur lies in the Terai region of Nepal. The climate there is usually very hot. But your flight will be amazing if the weather gets clear. Your flight will be around 45 minutes. As you reach Bhadrapur you will furthermore have an hour drive and reach Illam.
Overnight at Ilam
- Day 04: Drive From ILam to Sekhathum (1600m)
Today will be a long day’s drive. Today your destination will be sekhathum (Taplejung). You will be traveling through a busy road and be introduced to amazing landscapes. Your drive will be 8-10 hours.
Overnight at Sekhathum.
- Day 05: Trek from Sukathum to Amjilosa (2,308 m /7,570 feet) 6-7 hours6
Today your trail goes through the dense forest of pine and juniper. You will come across a suspension bridge and waterfall along your trail. Amjilosa is a small village populated by the Sherpa community people. Your trek will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Amjilosa
- Day 07: Trek from Amjilosa to Gyabla (2,730 m /8,954 feet) 6-7 hours
Your trek starts as you finish your breakfast. You will witness from this point the most people you meet are much influenced by Buddhist and Tibetan lifestyle. Trails are flavored with pine, bamboo, oak, and rhododendron trees.
You will encounter Himalayan Yak, goats, and Sheep including various species of birds. Your trek will be 6-7 hours long.
Overnight at Gyabla
- Day 08: Trek from Gyabla to Ghunsa (3,595 m/11,792 feet) 5-6 hours
Today your destination will be Ghunsa. Ghunsa is a large village of Sherpa community people. You will find many hotels and shops in Ghunsa. You will cross through a village called Phale which used to be a Tibetan camp many decades ago when Tibetan flee to Nepal from this trail from Tibet.
The people from higher villages come to Ghunsa for settlement during winter to escape from the cold.
Overnight at Ghunsa
- Day 09: Acclimatisation day at Ghunsa
Acclimatization is an Extra Day where you will be introduced to a new elevation but the overnight will be at the same spot. It is done so to keep your body familiar with the new environment and to escape from Altitude sickness.
Acclimatization Day will play a vital role to prevent you from Altitude sickness. Today you can visit the local villagers’ homes, learn their language, culture, and tradition.
Overnight at Ghunsa
- Day 10: Trek from Ghunsa to Kambachen (4,050 m/13,284 feet) 5-6 hours
As you finish your breakfast, your trek starts towards Kambachen. Today you will have a short trek for around 4 hours. Today you will follow the river which flows towards you. Today will be the first day to witness mountains very close to you. You will see different peaks dominated by Mt. Janu 7711m. This mountain is also well known as Mt. Kumbakarna, Mt. Jongsong peak 7462m, Tangkogma 6224, and many more.
Overnight at Kambachen
- Day 11: Acclimatisation Day at Kambachen (4,050 m/13,284 feet) 5-6 hours at Kambachen
Another day to acclimatize your body and keep yourself away from altitude sickness. Today you can rest and explore the village or else climb a nearby ridge which gives an amazing view of Mt.Kanchanjunga.
Overnight at Kambachen
- Day 12: Trek from Kambachen to Lhonak (4,780 m/15,678 feet) 6-7 hours
Now the elevation has increased a lot. You will feel the absence of trees and vegetation. You will find shrubs and high altitude of grass and flowers. Your trail will be over moraine formed by Glacier of Mt. Kanchanjunga. If you are lucky enough and have a binocular with you, a snow leopard can sometimes be spotted. Uphill trek from 4-5 hours long.
Overnight at Lhonak
- Day 13: Trek to Pangpema/Kanchenjunga North Base Camp (5,388 m/17,673 feet) and back to Lhonak6-7 hours
As you finish your breakfast, your trek continuous towards the North Kanchanjunga Base Camp. Your today’s trek will be 6-7 hours long.
You will have an amazing view of Mt.Kanchanjunga 8586m, Mt. Yalung Kang 8505m, Mt. Kangbachen 7903m, Mt.Nepal Peak 7177m, Mt.Ramthang Chang 6750m, and Mt Gimmigela Chuli I 7305m and more.
After a memorable time at the northern base, you will trek back to Lhonak.
Overnight at Lhonak
- Day 14: Trek back from Lhonak to Ghunsa(3,595 m) 6-7 hours long walk
Today you will descend back to Ghunsa Village. The trail will be used the same which was used for ascending to Northern Base Camp. Today your Trek will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Ghunsa
- Day 15: Trek from Ghunsa to Sele Le (4,290 m/14,071 feet) 5-6 hours
Your trek starts as usual after breakfast. Today will be 5-6 hours long Trek. Your trails will be through dense forest climb through the grassy ridge. From distance, you will have a view of Phale and Ghunsa Village along with Mt. Makalu’s insight.
Overnight in Sele le
- Day 16: Trek from Sele Le to Cheram (3,870 m/12,694 feet) 6-7 hours
A day to trek over high hill ridges. Today you will walk through Sinon La and Sinelapche la Pass. Amazing views of different peaks around, the valley below, and Kanchanjunga can be seen astonishingly.
After climbing high ridges then we descend towards Cheram. It is mandatory to reach Cheram as it is the only place where you will find a human settlement on today’s trail.
Overnight at Cheram
- Day 17: Trek from Cheram to Ramche (4,580m) 4 to 5 hours
Today you walk from Cheram to Ramche for Kanchenjuga south base camp
- Day 18: Hike to Oktang(4,730m) and Back to Cheram 6 to 7 hours
Today hike to oktang and back to again Cheram.
OverNight at Cheram
- Day 19: Trek from Cheram to Tortang(2,995m) 4 to 5 hours
Today we back from Cheram to Tortang and overnight at Tortang.
Over night at Tortang
- Day 20: Tortang to Yamphudin (2,080 m/6,822 feet) 6-7 hours
Today your destination is Yamphudin 2080m. Yamphudin is a small Sherpa village on the hillside. You will find small stupas and mani along the trail. This route is usually used by the expedition groups of the Mt. Kanchanjunga expedition. Your trail is normal to descend through the woods.
Overnight at Yamphudin
- Day 21: Drive From Yamphuding to Ilam (7 to 8 hours)
Today you will have a drive from Yamphuding to Ilam. The weather will be comparatively hot. Today will be a long day’s drive. Your drive will be 7 to8 hours long.
Overnight at Ilam
- Day 22: Drive from Bhadhrapur to then Fly to Kathmandu
Today you will have a morning drive to Badrapur. Your flight ticket from Bhadrapur to Kathmandu will be booked for the afternoon. Hence you will arrive in Badrapur for lunch and then fly to KTM.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 23: Free Day in Kathmandu
As mentioned above this day is specially programmed to recover if the Badrapur- Kathmandu flights get delayed due to uncertain changes in weather.
With timely arrival, you can Explore the Thamel market and shop for your family. You can also make a short city tour to explore some World Heritage Site.
You will be invited for the Last farewell dinner with Sherpa Himalaya Executive.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 24: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone, and a certificate for your completion ofKanchanjunga Circuit Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
