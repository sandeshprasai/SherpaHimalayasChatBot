Experience standing on the edge of the Tibetan Plateau, its untouched century-old socio-cultural practices, historically rich archeological sites, and magnificent landscapes throughout the rain shadow region in the 4WD Jeep Mustang Overland Tour. Sherpa Himalaya designs a special package as an alternative to Trekking to include all kinds of travelers to the isolated and pristine land of Mustang, all in a short period of time.
The fun-filled and full of adventure safari trip commences from the beautiful city of Pokhara. Moving through the winding roads we reach Beni, from where the off-road starts. Kicking the dust through the off-road the cruiser goes along the barren George, magnificent cliffs, and various passes. Mustang Overland Tour starts with a ride through the Kaligandaki George; the world’s deepest, with the majestic Dhaulagiri and Annapurna Massifs in the background. The fascinating Jeep tour ultimately takes you to Lo-Manthang. You will have enough time to explore the diverse culture and topography of the Upper Mustang. And travel back through the same route.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Overland Trip Preparation Day
Today you will have a free day to prepare your necessary gear and permits for the Overland Trip.
- Day 03: Drive from Kathmandu to Pokhara by Jeep/Car
Today we will have a long drive to Pokhara from Kathmandu. Your drive will start early in the morning, with 6- 7 hours’ time depending upon the traffic condition.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 04: Drive from Pokhara to Kagbeni (2,800m)
Today your drive starts early in the morning. This day will be a long day drive with an introductory offroad. Your overnight destination will be Kagbeni.
Kagbeni is a traditional village of mostly Gurung people living there. You will find beautiful small villages of mud. The landscape is totally different compared to other locations.
Overnight at Kagbeni
- Day 05: Drive from Kagbeni to Ghami (3,510m)
With a beautiful morning breakfast we will head towards Ghami.
Our Trails will be beside the kaligandagi river bank as well as through the river as some point.
Ghami is the mind point of two trails; Drakmar and Charang
Overnight at Ghami
- Day 06: Drive from Ghami to Lo Manthang ( 3,840m)
Today our Drive will be last point of Upper Mustang Trek; Lo manthang
We will be in Lo manthang by afternoon. China border is very close from Lo manthang so, you are requested to travel only with the guide.
Drones are also very strictly prohibited in this region.
Overnight at Lo manthang
- Day 07: Exploration Day in Lo manthang and Chhosar cave
Lo-manthang is the last point of the Tour. It is elevated at an altitude of 3700m. Today we will explore of Lomanthang. We will visit some monasteries around and the Royal king palace.
There are monasteries which are almost built before 15 centuries. The Royal palace was itself built during 1700’s
We can enjoy and explore beautiful traditional coffee shops around.
Overnight at Lo manthang
- Day 08: Drive back to Jomsom
As we finish our breakfast we will head back to Jomsom. Today we will have long drive. We will reach Jomsom by afternoon.
Overnight at Jomsom
- Day 09: Drive back from Jomsom to Pokhara
As earlier today you will have an early morning drive back to Pokhara from the same trail.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 10: Rest Day in Pokhara.
A pleasant day to take a rest close to the lakeside. Explore beautiful Pokhara City/lakesides with some world heritage sites.
Pokhara provides adventures such as Bungee / Paragliding. If you are interested for any adventure activities prior information to Company will be much appreciated.
Overnight at Pokhara.
- Day 11: Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu
Today you will Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by a Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Upper Mustang Jeep Tour, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free time to explore and shop for your family, or else you can also make a short city tour.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 12: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Upper Mustang Jeep Tour with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
