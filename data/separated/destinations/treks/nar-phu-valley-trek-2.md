Shortest Annapurna Base Camp Trek – 10 Days, Annapurna base camp trek is an amazing walk...
Nar-Phu valley with Thorongla Pass Trek is a fantastic journey to the wild and unexplored vicinity located north of Annapurna, this rugged and wild trek takes us to the exotic and enthralling valley of Nar Phu. Nar-phu valley trek starts from Jagat following the popular trekking routes of Annapurna Circuit trail and then branch off from Koto to follow the off the beaten track to Nar-Phu valley in the north of Manang and pass the Kang La pass with a magnificent panorama of Annapurna massifs. The Kang La pass links Nar with Manang and three other Bhotia villages in the Neyshang valley. The valley system above opens up to a huge expanse of high snow-peaks, ancient villages, and high altitude grazing settlements. Trekking to this hidden mountain valley offers medieval Tibetan culture in undisturbed form, dramatic mountains view and great alpine sceneries. We then head west over the Thorong La Pass and down through lower Mustang before heading on to Jomsom. We finally fly back to Pokhara and drive back to Kathmandu.
Accommodations
As you are picked from Tribhuwan International Airpot, Kathmandu you will be accommodated in a three-star level Hotel with Bed and Breakfast. We accommodate on the basis of twin shared room. A single room can be also accommodated upon your request. Your room will be facilitated with attached restrooms in the Kathmandu. During the Trekking premises, in some places, they only have a shared washroom and restroom. As you reach higher elevation single rooms may be difficult to manage.
Meals
During our Trek Authentic Nepali food is available and more common. You will also get international Cuisine such as Continental, Italian, Indian, etc. Breakfast-Dinner will be served as per lodge menu where we stay overnight. Lunch will be served on the trail of Next destination. During Trek, all meals will be provided whereas, only breakfast is served in Kathmandu. Sherpa Himalaya will serve you special last day dinner.
Health Condition & Fitness Requirement
This Trek is a moderate level Trek. You will be walking for 6-7 hour a day on average. We will face the steep hill, cross steep, hilly terrain etc. We will be heading mostly uphill where we will face an average of 4000m altitude change. Altitude problem will be negligible due to proper acclimatization day . If you have strong determination and hope in yourself then this Trek will not be that difficult.
If you have done any Trek before it will be your skill but you don’t require pre-experience or any training for this Trek. And if you have any health problems it would be better if you consult with a doctor before your visit and give the details to Sherpa Himalaya so that we could customize your trip as per your body requirement.
Best Time to Visit
Weather in Nepal is quite a favorable. For trekking we have two peak season; Spring & Autumn. Autumn season starts from September to November whereas Spring season starts from March to May. Beside this two season, you can visit Nepal in other months too but it is quite cold in January and rainy in the month of July & August.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival in kathmandu ( 1300m)
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trek Preparation day
we will gather to discuss in brief, the tour itinerary and other information. We will then check clothing and trekking equipment you will require during our travel.You will be taken to shop if you need to buy some gears.Overnight at hotel.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 03: Kathmandu to Besisahar and drive to Dharapani (1960m )
As we finish our breakfast we will take a private vehicle and drive till Dharapani which will almost take 9-10 hours. You will be able to witness the real geography and landscape of Nepal. We will be going via Besi sahar.
Overnight at Dharapani
- Day 04: Trek to Koto (2610m)
As we finish our Breakfast we will head towards Koto. It will around 5-6 hours. As we Trek ahead for few hours we will have our lunch at Timal most probably.
our Trail will be easy this day and we will reach Koto almost between 3-4 pmOvernight at Koto
- Day 05: Trek to Meta (3560m)
Today we will have a Trek almost all away through the dense Jungle. As always we will be heading ahead as we finish our Breakfast. If you are lucky enough you might be able to see some of the rare birds and animals of Nepal. We will face uphill way as we are near to Meta. Overnight stay at Meta.
Overnight at Meta
- Day 06: Trek to Phu-Gaun (4250m)
This Day we will make our Trek almost for 7-8 hours. This although we will have longer Trek our Trail will not be much difficult. We will be passing through plain barren land with very less flora and fauna. We will face very gradual uphill. Tonight we will stay overnight at Phu Gaon.
Overnight at Phu Gaun
- Day 07: Acclimatazation day in phu-gaun
This Day is kept so that our body will get properly acclimatize with the altitude face. Although it is a free day we will have short hike around which can be upto 500m high walk and we will again come back to Phu Gaon for overnight.
Overnight at Phu Gaon
- Day 08: Trek Back to Nar (4110m)
As we finish our rest or acclimatization Day we will head towards Nar. We will Trek for around 7-8 hours. Today we will come back nearby Meta and then cross the Meta river and make our Trail slightly uphill and reach Nar. Overnight Stay at Nar.
Overnight at Nar
- Day 09: Trek to Ngawal Via Khangla Pass (5,322m)
As usual after we finish our Breakfast we will be ready to make our Pass; Kangla Pass. This pass is a moderate level pass and will not be much difficult. You will be properly trained so that you can easily finish this pass. As we complete our pass we will descend down to reach till Ngwal. It make take 7-8 hours to reach Ngwal. Today we will have overnight stay at Ngwal.
Overnight at Ngwal
- Day 10: Trek to Manang (3540m)
Today we will have a Short Trek to Manang. Our Trail will be of around 4-5 hours. We will be descending down and then straight forward from the half way to reach Manang. Manang is one of the major city nearby where you will find good accommodation facilities. Overnight at Manang
Overnight at Manang
- Day 11: Trek to Yak Kharka( 4,100m)
We will start our Gradual trek from Manang village via a valley of Jarsang Khola. The trail portrays pastures, juniper trees along will gradual elevation. Our route will cross Ghunsa village and will lead us to grazing meadows of horses and yaks. We will take every measure to ensure that every member is safe from altitude sickness. We will stay overnight at Yak Kharka.
Overnight at Yak Kharka
- Day 12: Trek To Thorang High Camp (4,800m)
Throang High Camp is at an elevation of((4800m/15748 ft)which will be our destination for this day. It will be an uphill trek. We will cross a river with suspension bridge and reach Thorang Phedi. Thorang High Camp is the last Tea house before Thorang La Pass. Overnight in Thorang High Camp.
Overnight at High Camp
- Day 13: Trek to Muktinath (3,800m) Via ThoRang La Pass (5,416m)
Thorong La pass (5,416m) is one of the highest passes in the world, which is difficult to cross and will be our ultimate objective today. We will be using the route from Muktinath to Jomsom as it is assumed to be safest. We will use early morning time to cross the pass and reach Muktinath valley at foot of the pass. Overnight at Muktinath.
Overnight at Muktinath
- Day 14: Trek To Jomsom
If we prefer to drive to Jomsom then it will take about an hour. But we suggest trekking as it is more fun than driver. We will pass via deserted villages and reach Jomsom for lunch. We will explore local area and stay overnight at Jomsom.
Overnight at Jomsom
- Day 15: Jomsom to Pokhara
Today as we finish our Breakfast we will either reach Pokhara by flight or by drive. It will take around 20min to reach Jomsom by flight whereas 7-8 hours by drive.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 16: Pokhara to Kathmandu
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Narphu Valley with Thorong la Pass Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free day as a day to explore and shop for your family or else you can also make short city tour.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 17: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Narphu Valley with Thorong la Pass Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
