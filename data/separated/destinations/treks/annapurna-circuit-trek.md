Shortest Annapurna Base Camp Trek – 10 Days, Annapurna base camp trek is an amazing walk...
Annapurna Circuit Trek; completes in 15 Day which is the best itinerary. For the reason that, your body acclimatized and keep you safe all way of the Trek. The highest altitude you will face during this Trek is 5416m.
The highest elevation 5416m is of Thorong-la Pass. On this Annapurna Circuit Trek we accomplish this Trek through anti-clockwise route as it is shorter. This helps in proper acclimatization to your body and this direction pass is easier and safer.
It introduce you to the great Himalayas of Nepal which is almost, above <6000m elevation. The Mountains you encounter on the way are Annapurna Massif (Annapurna I-IV), Manaslu, Paungda Danda, Tilicho Peak, Machhapuchhre, Gangapurna, Pisang Peak, and Dhaulagiri.
This Trek is a bit challenging as it passes through small obstacles such as bridge, rivers and narrow as well as wide ways. Besides that, proper hotel and motel facilities are available for meals and accommodation in this Annapurna Circuit Trek.
Annapurna Circuit Trek covers subtropical forests, many waterfalls, and gigantic cliffs, with small villages. This region populates mainly Gurung, Manang and Thakali people.
Similarly, Thakali peoples dish is also consider as the best dish on Trek. This Trek can be long up to 20 days but can be minimize if travel speed is good enough, if you travel more on a vehicle or take direct flight while returning or going.
Long time back people use to Trek right from Besisahar but due to, access of proper transportation many people start this Annapurna Circuit Trek from Dharapani(1920m/6299 ft) and some from Chame(2630m/8629 ft). The best month to Trek is October-November as everything in this circuit gets refreshed by the recent monsoon season. during this season views of mountains are very clear and skies at night are extremely visible.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival day in Kathmandu (1300m)
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Drive from Kathmandu to Besi Sahar (1100m/3608 feet), and drive to Dharapani 8 – 10 hours
Today we will have a long day drive. As we finish our breakfast we will head towards Dharapani via Besisahar. It will take 6 Hours to drive to besisahar by private vehicle. After short rest we will again have to drive 2 hours long offroad to reah afternoon camp Dharapani.
This drive will be very interesting and perfect time to know more about the landscape of Nepal.
Overnight at Dharapani
- Day 03: Dharapani(1860m) to Chame (2,710m/8,891feet), 5 – 6 hours
Today is our first Trek day. We will start our Trek from Dharapani, heading towards Chame which is elevated at altitude of 2710m.
Your trek will be 5-6 hours long along the river side enjoying beautiful nature and mountain such as Mt. Lamjung and Mt. Annapurna
Overnight at Chame
- Day 04: Trek from Chame to Pisang (3,300m/10824feet), 5 – 6 hours
After we finish our breakfast we will head towards Pisang. The trek will be normal with slight slopes. It will take Normally 5 hours to reach Pisang.
You will find two Pisang; Upper Pisang and Lower Pisang. The camp site will be decided by then while we get close to. Both the Pisang are nearby so, it is similar wherever we stay for camp. You can witness Mt. Annapurna II directly from Pisang
Overnight at Pisang
- Day 05: Trek from Pisang to Ngawal (3700m/12139 ft) 4-5 hours
Today you will have a normal trek with an almost 2-hour uphill climb. As you complete the uphill climb you will be introduced to one of the oldest and traditional villages of the Annapurna Region; well known as Gyaru.
After a short break at Gyaru with a furthermore, 1 and half hour normal trek you will reach Ngawal. One of the best locations providing you breathtaking views of Mt. Annapurna II, IV, II, Mt. Gangapurna, Mt. Tilicho, Mt. Chulu East, Far East, and Mt. Pisang.
Overnight at Ngawal
- Day 06: Trek from Ngawal to Manang (3,500m/11,482feet), 3-4 hours
Today we will reach a beautiful small village called Manang. Manang is also the perfect location to acclimatize your body to altitude. Today you will have a normal descend and sideways trek.
With normal 3-4 hours of Trek, we will reach Manang.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 07: Acclimatization day in Manang: hike to chongkor view point (3750m)
Today is the acclimatization day. This day is specially kept to let your body acclimatize with the altitude and the environment. You can take this day as a rest day too.
This day is also known as hike up sleep low. Today we will hike to Chongur View Point crossing through Gangapurna Glacier. you will also encounter beautiful glacier lake.
After we finish our exploration around we will come back to camp and be prepared for the next day.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 08: Trek from Manang to Yak Kharka (4,110m/13,484feet), 3 - 4 hours
After a day rest we will now again our Trek routine as usual. We will head ahead after having our breakfast. Today our destination is Yak Kharka.
Yak Kharka is a small village located between two big hills. Today we will have a short Trek which usually takes 4 hours. We will be there at yak Kharka for lunch and explore around.
Overnight at Yak kharka
- Day 09: Trek from Yak kharka to Thorang High Camp(4800m/15748 ft) : 5-6 hours
Today your Trek will be more longer then the past day. We are now closed to Thorongla Pass. Our today’s camp will be below the pass; which is also known as thorongla pass.
It will normally Take 6-7 hours , but the trail is not much hard. We will hike normally with gentle slope and with normal uphills.
Overnight at Thornag High Camp
- Day 10: Trek from High Camp to Muktinath (3,800/12,467ft) via Thorong La pass (5416m/17764feet)
Today is the day to reach upto your final & main destination of this entire Trek. Its dream of millions but only few take this encouraging trek and make their life filled with beautiful memories and adventure.
We will trek as we finish our breakfast. We will trek uphill until we reach Thorongla Pass (5416m). The trail is not much difficult.
After continuous 3-4 hours climb of uphill slopes we will reach at Thorongla Pass. which is also the main attraction of Annapurna Circuit Trek. We will enjoy our successful Trek there and take some beautiful pictures capturing the memories.
This point can let you view two big and famous trekking regions of Nepal. Annapurna Region and Mustang region.
Now after the pass we will enter into mustang region. Today our stay will be at Muktinath (3800m). It will take normally 2 hours downhill.
Overnight at Muktinath
- Day 11: Trek or drive to Jomsom, 5-6 hours trek, 1 hour drive
Now we will head for the another beautiful attraction Ghorepani Poonhill. Today our camp will be Jomsom. We will either trek or drive to Jomsom.
It will normally take 5-6 hours by trek and 1 hour by drive. If you decide to trek down then you will visit one of the most historic village in mustang Village known as Kagbeni.
You will witness small mud houses and old temples with beautiful tibetan lifestlye. your trek will be a worth decision going through Kagbeni.
Overnight at Jomsom
- Day 12: Drive from Jomsom to Tatopani (2,700m/8,860feet)
As we finish our Breakfast today we will Head towards Tatopani. Which again falls in Annapurna Region. Today we will have a day long drive which will take normally 6-7 hours.
After this adventurous drive we will reach Tatopani. You will see beautiful landscape around and also get knowledge that how different geographically Nepal is.
Overnight at Tatopani
- Day 13: Trek from Tatopani to Ghore Pani (2,750 m/ 9,020 feet)
Today we will Trek to Tatopani. It will have a hard Trek then usual. You will have to make trek uphill through the forest. It will take almost 5 hours uphill Trek.
They say the best view comes after the hardest climb. We will reach one of the most attractive destination of Annapurna Region. This point is also known as Ghorepani Poonhill Trek which reflects highlight of Annapurna.
Overnight at Ghorepani
- Day 14: Early morning sunrise view from Poonhill (3210m) and Trek from Ghore Pani to Hile, and Drive to Pokhara 5-6 hours
Today early morning, we will hike one hour long uphill to Poonhill. Poonhill lies at an elevation of (3210m). You will have amazing sunrise view over the Annapurna Himalayan Range.
As we enjoy the breathtaking sunrise view of Annapurna region we will come back to Ghorepani and then hike upto hile. It will take normally 3 hours.
We will have our lunch and then take our drive to Pokhara.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 15: Drive / Flight from Pokhara to Kathmandu, 5-6 hour
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Annapurna Circuit Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free time to explore and shop for your family or else you can also make short city tour.
- Day 16: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Annapurna Circuit Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
