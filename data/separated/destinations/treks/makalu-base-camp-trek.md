Sherpani Col Pass Trek is an expedition from Makalu Base Camp to Everest via Sherpanicol Pass,...
Like some of the less frequent areas in Nepal, Makalu Base Camp Trek also appeals to solo trekkers, who also want to be entertained and be under the influence of nature that has extraordinary beauty, and whose trekking trail takes you to the place, where the world’s fifth highest mountain is located, this trail also leads you through unfamiliar regions, where you are face-to-face with the people, who follow the long established traditions that your ancestors have left far behind by embracing modern technology and modern thoughts, but wherein the perfect relation between the plants and animals exist in a harmony that the whole world wants to see once more along with you, and what looks here is everything is unchanged.
Makalu Base Camp Trek takes place in the Makalu Barun National Park, where the trekking gets wilder as the forest closes in, where the distant lush green hill welcomes you, which is a perfect habitat for different species of birds, including the Wren Babbler, that is rarely found in other parts of the world, and here, more than 75 species of mammals have adapted to this place since time immemorial, including Red Panda, Snow-Leopard and Musk Deer, and they may come across your eyes if you are fortunate. These rare animals thrive in the cruel climate of the mountains, and this is how they become legendaries, and to have their glimpse is rewarding itself. The lower region of the park is covered with rhododendron forests, while the upper constitutes of granite cliffs, hanging glaciers, and waterfalls, and both seem to push their limits to show you different aspects of the Himalayan landscapes, and Makalu Base Camp, which is an ultimate place, offers you a breath-taking view of mountains, such as Kanchenjunga massif, Everest, Lhotse, Baruntse, Chamlang and others, and what could be better in your life than to see these mountain giants and their summits.
The common places that the trekking trail takes you are uphills and downhill and of course the valleys, alpine lakes, and glaciers, shaped by geological affects. Above all, the trekking trail takes you to the heart of the Makalu Region.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trekking preparation day in kathmandu. Stay overnight at Hotel in Kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Makalu Base Camp Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and the rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Fly from Kathmandu to Tumlingtar and stay at Hotel
Today you will need to wake up early in the morning. You will be notified by Sherpa Himalaya about the time. You will be received by a private vehicle and dropped at to Airport together with the Guide. You will have a very amazing Flight to Tumlingtar from Kathmandu. You will witness very beautiful villages and Aerial view of Himalayan ranges around. With clear weather, you will have a clear idea about the Landscape of the Hilly and Himalayan region.
If the flight’s lands earlier to Tumlingtar and have enough time we can drive to Num which takes around 3 and a half hour.
Overnight at Tumlingtar
- Day 04: Tumlingtar to Num 1560 m By Drive by Jeep
Today you will drive to Num by jeep. The drive today will be around 3 to 4 hours long. You will witness small villages and fields farmed with corn.
Your drive will be through Chicula which is elevated at 1900m. It is a small village having a police checkpoint. Here we have to present all your required documents at the checkpoint.
The roads are comparatively offroad from Chicula.
Overnight at Num
- Day 05: Trek from Num to Seduwa (1,500m/4922 ft): 6-7 hours
Num and Seduwa are at the same elevation. These two villages are opposite each other separated by the Arun River which is 800m below both villages.
You can clearly understand what trail looks like today. Your trail starts with descending between the thin village and cash crops; cardamom.
As you reach to Arun River you again climb to another side of the hill towards Seduwa. During your ascent you will encounter bustling villages lives, bamboo forest. The day will be quite hot. Today your trek will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Seduwa
- Day 06: Trek from Seduwa to Tashigaon (2,100m/6890ft): 4-5 hours
A beautiful day to trek. Today your trekking trail is quite easier than the day before. You will have a normal uphill climb introducing stepped stone trails and stone carved with Buddhist prayers.
Bamboo forest, Rhododendron forest, and Cardomom plantation are mostly seen during your trek. Tashigaon is a small village on the hillside. You will find mostly Sherpa community people residing here. It takes 5-6 hours of trek to reach Tashi Gaon.
Overnight at Tashigaon
- Day 07: Trek from Tashigaon to Khongma Danda (3,500m/11,483 ft): 6-7 hours
As you finish your breakfast, your trek starts ascending through the stepped rocky trail. Today will be a tough day. Although you have a long climb you will be entertained by birds singing through the forest.
If you trek this trail during trail you would love to walk into a thick Rhododendron forest. The trails are made beautiful by the blossom of different colored rhododendron flowers and magnolia with snow-capped mountains towards the eastern side.
Your uphill climb through the forest continues. You will encounter big rocks marked by Buddhist flags. If the trail is covered with snow it can be a tricky trail often. You will need around 7 hours uphill climb today
Overnight at Khongma Danda.
- Day 08: Acclimatization / Rest
Acclimatization is an Extra Day where you will be introduced to a new elevation but the overnight will be at the same spot. It is done so to keep your body familiar with the new environment and to escape from Altitude sickness.
Acclimatization Day will play a vital role to prevent you from Altitude sickness. Today you can climb up to pass 3890m to introduce yourself to a new elevation. It takes around an hour to reach to pass. After a short time at the hilltop with a view of many snow-capped peaks along with Mt. Kanchenjunga 8586m, Mt. Makalu, Mt.Yalung, and many more you will descend down to Khogma Danda.
Overnight at Khongma Danda
- Day 09: Trek from Khongma Danda to Dobate 3860m : 6-7 hours
Today will be a tough day through high pass. Today your destination will be Dobate. It is very important that today you pack snacks along with you. You will find a small tea shop at Shipton La which cannot be sure to be open.
You will be introduced to lakes which are stunning at high altitude. Heading up through the stepped rocky trail you will come across Sano Pokhari at 4050m. It takes an hour to reach here from Kongma la Pass.
With continuous ascend you will pass over Shipton La 4200m. Before the pass, you will also come across a big lake called Thulo pokhari. Following descending trails up to Kalo Pokhara you make an easy climb and cross another pass called Keke la 4170m. With furthermore an hour descend you reach Dobate.
Overnight at Dobate
- Day 10: Trek from Dobate to Yangle Kharka (3,557m/11,670 ft): 6-7 hours
Today your trail begins with a steep descent into the Rhododendron forest which takes you closer to Barun River. As you cross the river your trail continuous underneath the land slid area.
You will need furthermore ascend which takes you to a place called Themathang Kharka 3500m. You will need almost 4 hours to arrive at this point. You will also meet a beautiful waterfall on the way to Themanthang. Continuing your trek for 1 and a half hours more you will arrive at Yangle Kharka.
Overnight at Yanglee Kharka
- Day 11: Trek from Yangle Kharka to Langmale Kharka: (4,410m/14,468ft): 5-6 hours
Today you will have an easier trek. You will have a normal uphill climb. You will come across stone mani carved with Buddhist prayers and flags. The trails are dominated by the stunning views of Peak 4, Peak 6, Yaupa, and Peak 7.
A beautiful day with snow-capped mountains, waterfalls, and sacred caves. With a normal uphill climb for 5-6 hours, you reach Langmale.
Overnight at Langmale
- Day 12: Trek from Langmale Kharka to Makalu Base Camp: (4,870m/15,978ft): 6-7 hours
A day to that takes you to your final destination. Today you will be at the Makalu Base Camp 4800m. Your trek starts with a normal trail combination of both ascend and descend. You will have a view of peaks 6 & 7 most of the time.
with a continuous climb towards Shershong 4630 m; a good camping site. Furthermore, climb the great Makalu comes to view. You will witness icefalls, crevasses, and glaciers along.
You will face bending trail and wishing to reach Makalu Base Camp. Continuing your trek passing through a small river you will be your most awaited destination; Makalu Base Camp Trek. You will have a clear view of Mt. Makalu 8485m and Mt. Makalu southeast 7860m. There is also a big glacier lake.
Overnight at Makalu Base Camp
- Day 13: Explore Makalu Base Camp
Today is a free day in Makalu Base Camp. You can spend this day by a climb to a hilltop of 5300m elevation from where you have a panoramic view of Mt. Everest, Mt.Makalu, Mt. Lhotse, Mt. Nuptse, Peak 4,6,7 and beautiful trails you have climbed towards.
Overnight at Base Camp
- Day 14: Trek from Makalu Base Camp to Yangle Kharka: 6-7 hours
Today we leave our dreamed destination Makalu Base Camp. A heavy heart to leave it behind along with the happiness that you have made your dreams come true. Your return trail is the same you climbed. After a 6-7 hours trek, you will arrive at Yangle Kharka.
Overnight at Yangle Kharka
- Day 15: Trek from Yangle Kharka to Dobate: 6-7 hours
Today you will follow the gentle meander and river passing through Rhododendron forest. You will have both ascend and descend. Your return from the same trail you had earlier.
Overnight at Dobate
- Day 16: Trek from Dobate to Khongma Danda: 6-7 hours
Today you will climb down from the high passed you made earlier. You will pass Keke-la, Shipton-la, Khongma-la. This is also the last day to see the mighty mountains of this region.
Descend for 6-7 hours long.
Overnight at Khongma Danda
- Day 17: Trek from Khongma Danda to Tashigaon: 4-5 hours
As you finish your breakfast, you start descending towards the Tashigaon. You will need 4-5 hours to reach Tashi gaon following the same trail.
Overnight at Tashigaon
- Day 18: Trek from Tashigaon to Seduwa: 4-5 hours
Today your return destination will be Seduwa. You will retrace the same trail for 4-5 hours long.
Overnight at Seduwa
- Day 19: Trek from Num to Tumlingtar: 5-6 hour trek
Today you will Trek to Num. On early arrival, you will drive to Num or else to Khandbari which is close to Tumlingtar. You will have a 5-6 hours walk and drive.
Overnight at Tumlingtar/Khandbari
- Day 20: Tumlingtar to Kathmandu: 50 mins flight
Today you will have a return flight from Kathmandu which will be around 50min. In clear weather, you will have an amazing view of the Himalayan Range.
Overnight at Katmandu Hotel
- Day 21: Free Day in Kathamandu
As mentioned above this day is specially programmed to recover if the Tumlingtar – Kathmandu flights get delayed due to uncertain changes in weather.
With timely arrival, you can Explore the Thamel market and shop for your family. You can also make a short city tour to explore some World Heritage Site.
You will be invited for the Last farewell dinner with Sherpa Himalaya Executive.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 22: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone, and a certificate for your completion of Makalu Base Camp with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
