Everest Panorama Trek; The Highlights of Everest Base Camp Trek. In this specific Trek, you will be traveling halfway to Everest Base Camp.
Tengboche Monastery is the final destination of the Trek which is at an elevation of 3,860 m (12,660 ft). This point gives an amazing panoramic view around the monastery.
Mt. Everest, Mt. Lhotse, Mt. Nuptse, Mt. Kwangde, Mt. Thamserku, Mt. Tawache, and Mt. Ama Dablam are the most Popular mountains; that you will be introduced to along with many small peaks during Everest Panorama Trek. This Trip is one of the most chosen trips by family groups with children.
This trek is specially programmed for travelers who wish to trek in Everest Region but have a very limited time period or for travelers having less physical fitness. Since the maximum altitude on the Trail of Everest Panorama Trek is only 3,860 m (12,660 ft); the chance of getting caught by altitude Sickness is rare.
Everest Panorama Trek starts a Direct flight from Kathmandu to Luka. During the flight, you will see many small villages and Himalayan Vistas. On a clear day with a morning flight, Aerial Sunrise is also expected. Similarly, You will Trek towards Namche Bazaar and stay a day off there with short hikes around for acclimatization and then slowly head towards Tengboche.
Tengboche Monastery was first built in 1916 AD. It is the most Devoted Buddhist monastery by Sherpa People. It was built by a monk with a strong connection to its mother monastery called Rongbuk Monastery which is situated in Tibet.
Tengboche Monastery got destroyed twice (Earthquake & Fire). It was again shaken by the 2015 Earthquake but today it stands still in the lap of the Himalayas giving important life-changing teachings to the 100’s and praying for Peace and Prosperity through out the world.
Until the reach of Tengboche, you will be introduced to many small stupas and gumbas throughout the trail. This Trail will surely give good knowledge and information about Buddhism. The trails are almost 80% covered by Sherpa People. Hence, many call the Everest Region “Homeland of Sherpa”.
You will get the opportunity to explore the lifestyle of Sherpa People and live with them together throughout the trial. Everest Panorama Trek enters into the Sagarmatha National Park from a Place called Monjo.
If you are lucky enough then you might see Red Panda on the trail which is one of the Endangered Animals of Nepal. You will witness beautiful flowers like Rhododendron (National Flower of Nepal), Blue Himalayan Anemone, Himalayan Oxeye Daisy, Monkshood, and many more.
You will also be introduced to an airport elevated at 11,290 ft (Shyangboche Airport), Largest Sherpa Village Khumjung, and Khunde.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival day in Kathmandu 1300m/4265 ft
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and explore around. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around.
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Everest Panorama Trek. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya
Overnight at KTM
- Day 02: Flight from Kathmandu – Lukla and Trek to Phakding 2,652m/8,700 ft, 3 – 4 hours
Today you will need to wake up early in the morning. You will be notified by Sherpa Himalaya about the time. You will be received by a private vehicle and dropped to Airport together with the Guide. You will have a very amazing Flight to Lukla from Kathmandu. You will witness Lovely villages and Aerial view of Himalayan ranges around. With clear weather, you will have a clear idea about the Landscape of the Hilly and Himalayan region.
As you arrive Lukla, we will take a short Rest and have breakfast or lunch according to the time of Arrival. After that, you will start your Everest Panorama Trek trail to Phakdhing. It will take around 2-3 Hours of Trek. You will make your overnight stay here.
Phakdhing is Elevated at 2610m/ 8562 ft. Before you arrive at Phakdhing the trail will be normal with some steep downhill and with normal uphill Trek. You will also have the first sight of Mountain Kusum Khangguru (6,369m) from Thado Koshi which is at height of 2580m.
Overnight at Lukla
- Day 03: Trek from Phakding – Namche Bazar 3440 m/11,280 ft, 5 – 6 hours
After a very comfortable stay at Phakdhing, we will climb up towards Namche Bazaar (3440 m/11,280 feet), Namche Bazar is a small town in Himalaya which used to be very famous trade center.
Today we will cross over the Hillary Bridge which is also one of the adventurous tasks to make. As we cross the Bridge we will hike straight uphill for almost 2 t0 3 hours to reach the overnight stop(Namche Bazaar). People used to get things from Tibet and sell making the main market point Namche. You will have plenty of hotels and souvenir shops. You can also get an option to buy any Trekking gears and necessary clothes you might need in case you missed any from Kathmandu or Luka.
During your ascent to Namche Bazar, you will encounter 5 Bridges to cross. This moment will also be one of the memorable. This Day will be a bit harder than the previous Day. You will face a normal uphill walk as well as steep uphill. With recent updates, the trails are made more proper and easier.
Overnight at Namche Bazaar
- Day 04: Acclimatization Day – Namche Bazaar, 3,440m/11,284 ft
Acclimatization is an Extra Day where you will be introduced to a new elevation but the overnight will be at the same spot. It is done so to keep your body familiar with the new environment and to escape from Altitude sickness.
Acclimatization Day will play a vital role to prevent you from Altitude sickness. This Day we will Hike up to Everest View Hotel which has an elevation of (3880m / 12729 ft.) It is also listed in the UNESCO World Heritage site.
You will encounter an amazing panoramic view of the Everest Region. Some of the popular Mountains are Mt. Lhotse, Mt Everest, Mt. Amadablam, Mt. Nuptse and Mt. Kongde, etc and many more.
We will return back to Namche for lunch and after lunch, you will have a free day and can explore the local place.
Overnight at Namche Bazaar
- Day 05: Trek from Namche Bazaar to Tengboche 3,860 m /12,660 ft.
This day after having breakfast we will head towards our Final destination Tengboche. As we climb the Namche hill we will make a gentle slope walk for around an hour and then descend down to have the Lunch at Pungi Thanga which has an elevation of 3260m.
After having a delicious lunch after good Trek we will have to climb up for around an hour and reach a very magnificent and beautiful attraction called Tengboche. There lies one of the oldest and largest monasteries in Everest Region. This location is also the last point of Everest Panorama Trek. Enjoy the magnificent location in the lap of the Great Mountains and make a lifetime memory here.
Overnight at Tengboche
- Day 06: Trek from Tengboche to Monjo 2835m/ 9301 ft
Today we will start to descend back. After having our Breakfast we will start trekking towards Monjo. It will take around 6-7 hours of walk.
Overnight at Monjo
- Day 07: Trek from Monjo to Luka
Today is the last Day of Trek. We will Trek down to Lukla. We will reach there by afternoon. You can enjoy the last day together with everyone.
Overnight at Lukla
- Day 08: Flight from Lukla –Kathmandu.
Today we will fly from Lukla to Kathmandu. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Everest Panorama Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel. An extra free day is programmed by Sherpa Himalaya. A free day is kept to recover if in case the flight gets delayed.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free day as a day to explore and shop for your family, or else you can also make a short city tour.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 09: Free Day in Kathmandu
As mentioned above this day is specially programmed to recover if the Lukla- Kathmandu flights get delayed due to uncertain change in weather.
With timely arrival, you can Explore the Thamel market and shop for your family. You can also make a short city tour to explore some World Heritage Site.
You will be invited for the Last farewell dinner with Sherpa Himalaya Executive.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 10: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-ta) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Everest Panorama Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
