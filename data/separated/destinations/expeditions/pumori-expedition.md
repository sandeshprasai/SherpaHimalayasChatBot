Mt. Pumori (7145 m) is located in the Khumbu Region of the Himalaya, Nepal. It is also the border between Tibet and Nepal. This Expedition starts from the Gateway of Khumbhu Regionl; which is Tenzing Hillary Airport, Lukla. Similarly, you will reach the Base Camp after approximate 7-8 days of Trek. Along the trail of this Expedition you will witness almost all the Mountains of Everest Region with breathtaking Landscapes
