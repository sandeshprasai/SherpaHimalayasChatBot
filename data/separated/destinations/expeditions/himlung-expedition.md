Mt. Himlung is located in the Northern East of Annapurna Range having an elevation of 7,126 m. Mt. Himlung is of of the secluded(hidden) mountain, Making border with Tibet. Mt. Himlung is also consider as a Technical Mountain to climb. Similarly, this expedition gives you a round trip of Annapurna towards Koto and reaching Nar Phu from Koto.
In addition, this expedition offers you amazing glaciers, springs and tall peaks. You will discover remote villages full of rare tradition and culture with their different lifestyle. You will have breathtaking view of many mountains which includes Mountains like Api Himal, Nandadevi Himal, Kappa Chuli Peak and a lot more.
Likewise, The Base Camp is situated at an elevation of 4900 m above the sea level. On this Expedition you will be having high three camps. The first High Camp is elevated at an altitude if 5450 m, second at 6000 m and similarly, the last one is at 6350 m. Despite of having steep and icy way after the third high camp, the summit is conquered by most. The normal time period for this expedition is of 10-13 days from Base Camp and back to base camp after summit.
