Chulu Far East Peak Climbing is one of the most exhilarating mountaineering programs in Nepal’s Annapurna...
Singu Chuli Peak or Fluted Peak located in the heart of Annapurna Range within the surroundings of Annapurna Sanctuary is an attractive mountain to climb from both a visual and climbing aesthetics. Singu Chuli is a fantastic looking peak that seems much more daunting viewed from Annapurna base camp than its height suggests. This impression grows the closer you get. Various routes are possible but the south ridge dominates the eye. Combined with Tharpu Chuli, its much easier neighbor, it makes a great and cheap proposition for serious Himalayan climbing in fantastic scenery without the crowds. It is a part of the ridge line extending south from Glacier Dome and sharing it with another attractive peak Tharpu Chuli or Tent Peak. This trek is strongly recommended for excellent mountain views. You will enjoy the great cultural diversity in Ghandurk, Chomorong & Ghorepani before entering Machhapuchhre (Fishtail) Base Camp. You can enjoy the panoramic views of the world’s highest mountains such as Annapurna I, Annapurna III and other snowy peaks.
All peaks must be climbed with a well climbing trained guide. Singu Chuli 6501m, formerly known as Fluted Peak is the highest amongst the “Trekking Peaks” of Nepal. Wilfred Noyce, the first man to climb this mountain with David Cox, compared its beauty with Indian’s highest and probably the most beautiful peak, Nanda Devi. This peak is situated in the Annapurna Sanctuary adjoined to Tharpu Chuli and across the Hinchuli peak.
We arrive at the Base camp of Singu Chuli after walking up the Modi Khola gorge to the Annapurna Sanctuary Base camp, at around 4350m and then establish two higher camps, one at 4900m at the approach of North East face and a higher one at 5500m, on the North East face of the mountain before the summit is gained. Vertical walls near the summit pose some technical challenge on this climb. This climb is suggested only to people with some alpine and ice climbing experience.
Safety is a major consideration during all our expeditions however there are dangers involved in climbing 6000m peaks that cannot be avoided. The extreme altitude, weather, cold and other mountaineering hazards ensure high altitude climbing has unavoidable risks at the best of times, members of our expeditions need to be aware of these dangers and accept that they are an integral part of high altitude mountaineering.

Day 01: Arrive in Kathmandu, transfer to hotel
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trek Preparation day in Kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Singu Chuli Climb. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase it from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and the rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Drive or flight to Pokhara (850m)/ Overnight at Hotel.
Today we will either drive or take a flight to Pokhara. For flight will take around 30-40 minutes depending upon the weather. If you take the option to travel by Vehicle then it will take around 8-9 hours normally. The time will take more if you get into traffic.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 04: Drive to Hile & trek to Uleri (1977m).
Today will be the first day of Trek. As we finish our breakfast we will have a short drive to Hile. It will take around one and half hour of Drive.
As we reach Hile we will start our Trek towards Ulleri. It will take 4-5 hours of Trek to reach Ulleri.
Overnight at Ulleri
- Day 05: Trek from Ulleri to Ghorepani (2855m) 4-5 hours walk
Today as we finish our breakfast we will head towards Ghorepani. With a normal uphill Trek of 4-5 hours through the rhododendron forest enjoying a small village you will reach Ghorepani in the afternoon.
This is also the last point of a short Trek called Ghorepani Poonhill Trek. This point provides you a beautiful Himalayan range of Annapurna Range mountains.
Overnight at Ghorepani
- Day 06: Trek from Ghorepani to Chuele (2,245m /7,365 feet) 6-7 hours
Today early in the morning we will take a short hike to Poonhill 3200m to enjoy the beautiful morning sunrise view over the Annapurna Range Himalayas.
After enjoying the beautiful time we will go back to Ghorepani and have our breakfast head towards Chuele.
Today the trek will be a bit longer. Your Trek will be 6-7 hours Trek.
Overnight at Chuele
- Day 07: Trek from Chuele to Sinuwa (2,300m /7,546 feet ) 6 -7 hour
Today we will head towards Sinuwa. The height of Sunuwa is 23oom and today the trail will be 6-7 hours long.We will start our Trek with normal descend and then we will cross a small bridge. Our lunch destination will be Chumrong which is reached with a normal slight uphill Trek.
As we finish our lunch we will head towards our overnight camp with again slight descend and crossing over the second bridge.
Today the Trek will be very interesting and nature friendly. Very safe trails through light jungle crossing rivers enjoying birds song.
Overnight at Sinuwa
- Day 08: Trek from Sinuwa to Deurali (3,200m /10,498 feet) 6-7 hrs
As we finish our breakfast we will start our Trek towards Deurali. The trail to Deurali is similar to the past day.We will pass through the normal forests crossing small rivers with slight descend and Ascend.
Our lunch will be at the Himalayan hotel. After a short rest, we will head for our Overnight stay location. As we head ahead we will encounter one of the biggest caves on the trail. This trail is known as Hinku Cave.
As we finish exploring our trail we will soon cross a small rive and then reach Deurali which lies to the next side of the hill.
Overnight at Deurali
- Day 09: Trek from Deurali to Machhapuchhre Base Camp (3700m/12135feet), 5- 6 hours
Today we will reach the base of Mt Machhapucchre. This destination has become a dream come true day for many people and a motivational, inspiring, and emotional destination for many. Today we will have a more uphill trek than earlier.Overnight at Machhapuchhre Base Camp
- Day 10: Acclimatization day at Machhapucchre Base Camp
Acclimatization is an Extra Day where you will be introduced to a new elevation but the overnight will be at the same spot. It is done so to keep your body familiar with the new environment and to escape from Altitude sickness.
Acclimatization Day will play a vital role to prevent you from Altitude sickness. This Day we will Hike up to Annapurna Base Camp which will take up to 2-3 hours. After a beautiful time at Annapurna Base Camp, you will return back to Machhapuchhre Base Camp overnight.
Overnight at Machhapuchhre Base Camp
- Day 11: Machhapuchhre Base Camp to Singu Chuli Base Camp (4350m).
From today your climbing days start. Your meal and accommodation will be in tents.
Climbing Period: Day 01
- Day 12: Base Camp to High Camp (4900m).
Climbing Period: Day 02
- Day 13: High Camp to Higher camp 1 (5500m
Climbing Period: Day 03
- Day 14: Higher Camp to Summit (6501m) and return to Base Camp.
Today will be the total climbing day. You will have to be prepared the previous day with all the gears and you start your climb at night time. Usually, your climb will be from 2-3 am. It is because as the sunrise there may be a risk of small avalanche, loosen of rocks due to melting of ice, and with a bright sun in the snow, it will definitely be a hard climb. The wind is also the major factor that you climb early in the morning. In the morning there will be very wind which makes it a lot easy and safe.
From the summit, you will witness many mountains which include Annapurna Range I-IV, Varah Shikhar 7647m, Khangsar Kang 7485m, Gangapurna 7485m, Annapurna South 7219m, Hiunchuli 6441m, and many countless small peaks
Taking beautiful pictures at the summit you then climb down and head to Singu Chuli Base Camp.
Overnight at Singu Chuli Base Camp
- Day 15: Base Camp to Machhapuchre B.C (3700m).
Today, with the celebration of the success of one of the most challenging and technical climbs in Annapurna you will climb down gradually towards Machhapuchhre Base Camp.
Overnight at Machhapuchhre Base Camp
- Day 16: Trek from Machhapuchre B.C to Bamboo(2,400m /7,874 feet ) 7 - 8 hours
Today you will return back towards the same trail which you used to climb. Today your destination will be Bamboo. Your trail will be downhill and takes normally 3-4 hours.Overnight at Bamboo
- Day 17: Trek from Bamboo to Jhinu Danda (1,760m/5770 feet), 5 - 6 hours
Today our camp will be at Jhinu Danda. Annapurna Base Camp Trek trail offers different trails for returning back which makes the trail more interesting and beautiful.As always after finishing our breakfast we will descend down towards Jhinu Danda. You will have to make an uphill climb to Chhomrong and again descend down to Jhinu. This beautiful place offers you a hot spring spot which gives you the perfect place for refreshment from the long Trek and climbs.
Overnight at Jhinu Danda
- Day 18: Trek from Jhinu Danda to Pokhara (823m/2,700feet), 4-6 hours
Finally today we will reach the capital town; Pokhara City. We will start our Trek down early morning and reach Siwai. Then we will drive to Pokhara by local jeep available there.Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 19: Drive or fly to Kathmandu (1350m) and transfer to Hotel
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by the Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped at Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Singu Chuli Climb, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free day as a day to explore and shop for your family, or else you can also make a short city tour.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 20: Free day in Kathmandu
Today will be a free day. Today you can prepare for your departure and also make a short city tour around and also use the day for shopping for some gifts for your family.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 21: Final Departure
Today morning your Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone, and a certificate for your completion of Singu Chuli Climb with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
