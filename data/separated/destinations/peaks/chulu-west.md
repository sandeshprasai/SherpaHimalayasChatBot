Chulu Far East Peak Climbing is one of the most exhilarating mountaineering programs in Nepal’s Annapurna...
Make your Chulu West Peak (6419m/21,055ft) and make the dream of peak climbing in one of world’s most beautiful regions (the Annapurnas) come true. There are two Chulu Peaks, East and West. This package is for the Chulu West Peak Climbing. Standing at a height of 6419m, the Chulu West Peak offers an interesting peak climbing opportunity for those looking for an adventure in the Annapurna Region.
Some consider the Chulu West Peak to be one of the best peaks in the Annapurna region. While climbing the peak, we will also be able to enjoy magnificient views of Dhaulagiri, Nilgiri, Annapurna, and other Himalaya peaks. However, climbing the Chulu West Peak requires a good physical condition and a high degree of stamina.
After initial preparations and sightseeing in Kathmandu, we drive to Khudi from where we begin our trek. The trail leads slowly to the Manang Valley, one of the highest valleys of the world. We set up two camps above the Base Camp.After summitting the Chulu West, we return via the Thorong-La Pass and Muktinath to Jomsom. From Jomsom, we fly to Pokhara and drive back to Kathmandu.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu and Transfer to Hotel - 1,300m/4,264 ft
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Preparation Day for Trekking
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide and Climber for Chulu West Peak. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase it from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and the rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Drive Kathmandu to Besi sahar and Drive to dharapani
Today we will have a long day drive. As we finish our breakfast we will head towards Dharapani via Besisahar. It will take 6 hours to drive to Besi Sahar by private vehicle. After short rest, we will again have to drive 2 hours long offroad to reach afternoon camp Dharapani.
This drive will be a very interesting and perfect time to know more about the landscape of Nepal.
Overnight at Dharapani
- Day 04: Dharapani-Chame [2,710m/8,891ft]: 6 - 7 hrs
Today is our first Trek day. We will start our Trek from Dharapani, heading towards Chame which is elevated at an altitude of 2710m.
Your trek will be 5-6 hours long along the riverside enjoying beautiful nature and mountain such as Mt. Lamjung and Mt. Annapurna
Overnight at Chame
- Day 05: Trek from Chame- Pisang [3,300m/10824ft]: 5 - 6 hrs
After we finish our breakfast we will head towards Pisang. The trek will be normal with slight slopes. It will take Normally 5 hours to reach Pisang.
You will find two Pisang; Upper Pisang and Lower Pisang. The campsite will be decided by then while we get close to it. Both the Pisang are nearby so, it is similar wherever we stay for camp. You can witness Mt. Annapurna II directly from Pisang
Overnight at Pisang
- Day 06: Pisang-Manang [3,500m/11,482ft]: 6 - 7 hrs
On the day 5th the trails are almost similar to the past day. Today we will reach a beautiful small village called Manang. Manang is also the perfect location to acclimatize your body with the altitude.
With normal 6-7 hours of Trek, we will reach Manang.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 07: Acclimatazation Day
Today is the acclimatization day. This day is specially kept to let your body acclimatize with the altitude and the environment. You can take this day as a rest day too.
This day is also known as the hike up sleep low. Today we will hike to Chongur View Point crossing through Gangapurna Glacier. you will also encounter a beautiful glacier lake.
After we finish our exploration around we will come back to camp and be prepared for the next day.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 08: Manang to Ledar [4200m/13,776ft]: 4 - 5 hrs
Your trail leads you towards the Annapurna Circuit trail. Today your camp will be in the middle of Yak Kharka and Thorung Phedi. Your destination today will be Ledar. A small village elevated ar 4200m. You will find an ACAP office at Ledhar where you will have to present all your documentation and permits. Today your climb will be 4-5 hours long.
Overnight at Ledar
- Day 09: Ledar to Chulu West Base Camp [4900m/16,072ft]: 4 - 5 hrs
Today from Ledar you leave Annapurna Circuit trail behind and head towards North-East which will lead you towards Mt. Chulu West. Today you will have a steep climb crossing landslide areas.
Your Trek will be short of 4-5 hours but quite challenging uphill.
Overnight at Chulu West Base Camp
- Day 10: Climb from Chulu West base camp to High Camp(5530m)
Now your trekking trails are over. Today you will have morning breakfast as start climbing towards Chulu West High Camp. You will have to put on your crampons and use fixed rope if necessary depending upon the trail condition. Your climb will be 4-5 hours long.
As you reach high camp you will have the final health check and gear inspection before heading towards the summit.
Overnight at High Camp
- Day 11: Climb from High camp to Chulu West Summit (6419m/21060ft) and Back to High Camp
Today will be the total climbing day. You will have to be prepared the previous day with all the gears and you start your climb at night time. Usually, your climb will be from 2-3 am. It is because as the sunrise there may be a risk of small avalanche, loosen of rocks due to melting of ice, and with a bright sun in the snow, it will definitely be a hard climb. The wind is also the major factor that you climb early in the morning. In the morning there will be very wind which makes it a lot easy and safe.
From the summit, you will witness Annapurna Range (I-IV) very close. Mt. Dhaulagiri 8167m, Mt. Annapurna range, Mt. Khangsar Kang, Mt. Gangapurna 7455m, Mt, Tarke Kang 7193m, Mt. Tilicho Peak 7134m, Mt. Himlung 7126m, Mt.Nilgiri 7061m, Mt Lamjung Himal 6893m, Mt. Tukuche 6920m, and many countless peaks can be seen.
Overnight at High Camp
- Day 12: Trek from High Camp to Ledar
As you finish your breakfast, you will descend towards Ledar. Celebrating the success of the climb today you will feel the trails much shorter and definitely easier after an adventurous climb. Your descend will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Ledar
- Day 13: Ledar to Thorong High Camp[4833m/15,8566ft]: 4-5 hrs
Today you will again join the Annapurna Circuit trail towards North-West. You will have a normal uphill climb towards Thorong Phedi. You will find a small tea shop in between and rarely some small houses. Today your climb will be 4-5 hours long.
Overnight at High Camp
- Day 14: Thorong High camp - Thorong Pass [5416m/17,765ft] - Muktinath [3,800/12,467ft]
Today is the day to reach up to your final & main destination of this entire Trek. It a dream of millions but only a few take this encouraging trek and make their life filled with beautiful memories and adventure.
We will trek as we finish our breakfast. We will trek uphill until we reach Thorongla Pass (5416m). The trail is not much difficult.
After a continuous 3-4 hours climb of uphill slopes, we will reach Thorongla Pass. which is also the main attraction of Annapurna Circuit Trek. We will enjoy our successful Trek there and take some beautiful pictures capturing the memories.
This point can let you view two big and famous trekking regions of Nepal. Annapurna Region and Mustang region.
Now after the pass, we will enter into mustang region. Today our stay will be at Muktinath (3800m). It will take normally 2 hours downhill.
Overnight at Muktinath
- Day 15: Trek from Muktinath to Jomsom 5-6 hours walk
Now we will head for another beautiful attraction Ghorepani Poonhill. Today our camp will be Jomsom. We will either trek or drive to Jomsom.
It will normally take 5-6 hours by trek and 1 hour by drive. If you decide to trek down then you will visit one of the most historic villages in mustang Village known as Kagbeni.
You will witness small mud houses and old temples with beautiful Tibetan lifestyle. your trek will be a worth decision going through Kagbeni.
Overnight at Jomsom
- Day 16: Jomsom to Pokhara by Flight
Today you will have a 30 min morning flight from Jomsom to Pokhara early in the morning. Jomsom gets hit by strong wind after 12 pm hence afternoon flight is not possible.
As you reach Pokhara in the morning before 10 am you use this day to explore the beautiful lakeside city. You can get adventure activities such as Bunjee and Paragliding in Pokhara. If you mention Sherpa Himalaya Team about the need for adventure activity then it would be appreciated and time will be managed accordingly.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 17: Drive back to Kathmandu: 6 - 7 hrs
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by the Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped at Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Pisang Peak Climb, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 18: Free Day in Kathmandu
Today will be a free day. Today you can prepare for your departure and also make a short city tour around and also use the day for shopping for some gifts for your family.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 19: Final Departure
Today morning your Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone, and a certificate for your completion of Chulu West with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
