Nima Dorji Sherpa
Trekking and Climbing GuideWith over 25 years of experience in the trekking and mountaineering industry, Nima Dorji Sherpa is a dedicated and highly skilled guide known for his kindness, support, and expertise. Throughout his illustrious career, he has successfully summited some of the most renowned peaks in Nepal, including Mt. Everest, Mt. Lhotse, Mt. Lhakpa-Ri, and numerous other high-altitude summits across the region.
As one of the senior guides at Sherpa Himalaya Treks and Expedition, Nima has been an integral part of our team since its inception, leading countless clients with unmatched passion and dedication. His extensive experience, combined with his deep commitment to client safety and success, makes him a trusted leader in the Himalayas.
Nima is fully certified in all essential training required for guiding clients in the challenging terrain of Nepal. His qualifications include:
- Trekking Guide Certification from the Government of Nepal (NATHM).
- Intensive Wilderness First Aid Training from Kathmandu Environmental Education Project (KEEP).
- Conservation and Biodiversity Training by the Trekking Agency Association of Nepal (TAAN).
- Mountaineering Course by the Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA).
- Basic Rock & Ice Climbing Training from the Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA).
Nima’s vast experience and qualifications ensure that he is well-prepared to provide a safe and rewarding trekking or mountaineering experience in the majestic Himalayas.
