Doma Sherpa
Operation ManagerDoma has been an indispensable part of Sherpa Himalaya Treks and Expedition, embodying the saying, “Behind every successful man is a woman making things work.” As a steadfast pillar of support to Mingma in both his personal and professional life, Doma shines as a strong, independent woman who entered the tourism industry at a young age and quickly made her mark. With her extensive experience leading numerous treks across Nepal and her expertise as a specialized Japanese trekking guide, she brings unparalleled charm, warmth, and professionalism to every endeavor.
As the Hospitality Partner of Sherpa Himalaya, Doma ensures every client feels at home, no matter how far they are from their own. She excels in coordinating accommodations by collaborating with hotels, lodges, and homestays to secure comfortable, budget-friendly options tailored to individual needs, including dietary preferences and accessibility. Her meticulous management of bookings, upgrades, and contingency plans guarantees a seamless experience, even during peak seasons or unexpected situations. Beyond logistics, Doma enhances the journey by curating personalized experiences—be it local recommendations, cultural dinners, or immersive activities that forge lasting memories.
A true advocate for sustainability and community empowerment, Doma actively promotes eco-friendly practices and fosters connections with local communities, artisans, and businesses. Through her efforts in marketing and destination promotion, she not only enriches the client experience but also upholds Sherpa Himalaya’s commitment to responsible tourism. With her unwavering dedication and magnetic personality, Doma is the heart of hospitality at Sherpa Himalaya, creating unforgettable journeys that resonate with authenticity and care.
