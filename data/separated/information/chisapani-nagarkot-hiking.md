The Chisapani and Nagarkot hike starts with a short drive to Sundarijal and enter into the Shivapuri National park, Nepal youngest National park and hike up to Chisapani that may take about
5-6 hours and you will be able to view many high Himalayas, hills, valley and we will hike to Nagarkot, the next day. Nagarkot is one of the most popular view points to view the sunrise
and sunset over the Himalayas as it is located at the altitude of 2211 meter above the sea level, if weather is good, we will see great views of Himalayas including Annapurna,
Langtang, Dorje Lakpa, Gauri Shanker, Ganesh Himal and some more. After spending an overnight in Nagarkot, we will hike down to Chagunarayan temple, an UNESCO heritage site and we will drive back to Kathmandu after visiting the Chagunarayan monuments.
