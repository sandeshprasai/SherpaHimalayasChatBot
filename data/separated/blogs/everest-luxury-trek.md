The best way to experience Nepal in a short period of time is to spend the 8 days walking holidays in the foothills of Mount Everest. Everest Luxury Trek run with a minimum of even one or two people and max. group size is 15 people and on dates of your choice: come with friends or family and trek at your own pace in comfort and style and enjoy your time, we take care of the rest.
This Everest luxury trek will take you all the way through the legendary Khumbu Everest region that offers miraculous views and the vigorous villages and ancient monasteries are captivating and encourage explorations.
Everest Base Camp luxury trekking is a dream come true for most of the adventurers but if you have only a short period of time, then this Everest Panorama view trekking up to a beautiful mountain and monastery town of Tengboche is the best hike for you. Tengboche is indeed a piece of Heaven with unique shaped Aamadablam mountain at the backdrop, view of Everest and you can do Meditation at an active monastery.
Everest Luxury Trek is a premium version of Everest Panorama Trek where you will be served with high service in the Mountain with best hotel at any overnight point starting from Lukla until the trek ends. You will be provided with best service in the remoteness of Mountain making you feel more comfort.
Everest region luxury trekking starts with a flight to Lukla and starts the amazing trek on the well-maintained trail that leads to Everest Base Camp that means you will already walk halfway to Everest base camp by the time you pass Namche and reach Tengboche. Along the way, you will come across many other adventurers who are taking the world famous Everest base camp trekking or even some of them climbing the highest mountain on Earth like Hillary and Tenzing in 1953.
From Namche, Syangboche, and Tengboche you will witness the Everest rising behind the Lhotse – Nuptse wall as well as the Kwangde, Tawache, Ama Dablam, Khangtega, and Thamserku. A panoramic Khumbu mountain range 3 of them over 8000 meters.
In addition, the welcoming Sherpa people whose hearts are life-size will enchant your memories for a lifetime… an amazing life-changing adventure and affordable trek in Nepal for you in style, we call it Everest Luxury trek.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu, Nepal
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your hotel of 4-5 stars level, where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Kathmandu Preparing Day
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Everest Base Camp Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Kathmandu - Lukla 2880 m - Phakding 2740 m
Today you will need to wake up early in the morning. You will be notified by Sherpa Himalaya about the time. You will be received by a private vehicle and dropped to Airport together with the Guide. You will have a very amazing Flight to Lukla from Kathmandu. You will witness very beautiful villages and Aerial view of Himalayan ranges around. With clear weather, you will have a clear idea about the Landscape of the Hilly and Himalayan region.
As you arrive Lukla, we will take a short Rest and have breakfast or lunch according to the time of Arrival. After that, you will start your Everest Base Camp Trek trail to Phakdhing. It will take around 2-3 Hours of Trek. You will make your overnight stay here.
Phakdhing is Elevated at 2610m/ 8562 ft. Before you arrive at Phakdhing the trail will be normal with some steep downhill and with normal uphill Trek. You will also have the first sight of Mountain Kusum Khangguru (6,369m) from Thado Koshi which is at height of 2580m.
Your overnight hotel will be at Yeti Mountain Home which is the best hotel at the location.
Overnight at Phakdhing
- Day 04: Phakding to Namche Bazaar
After a very comfortable stay at Phakdhing, we will climb up towards Namche Bazaar (3440 m/11,280 feet), Namche Bazar is a small town in Himalaya which used to be very famous trade center.
Today we will cross over the Hillary Bridge which is also one of the adventurous tasks to make. As we cross the Bridge we will hike straight uphill for almost 2 t0 3 hours to reach the overnight stop(Namche Bazaar). People used to get things from Tibet and sell making the main market point Namche. You will have plenty of hotels and souvenir shops. You can also get an option to buy any Trekking gears and necessary clothes you might need in case you missed any from Kathmandu or Luka.
During your ascent to Namche Bazar, you will encounter 5 Bridges to cross. This moment will also be one of the memorable. This Day will be a bit harder than the previous Day. You will face a normal uphill walk as well as steep uphill. With recent updates, the trails are made more proper and easier.
Your overnight hotel will be at Yeti Mountain Home which is the best hotel at the location.
Overnight at Namche Bazaar
- Day 05: Namche Bazaar – Everest View point – Namche Bazaar
Acclimatization is an Extra Day where you will be introduced to a new elevation but the overnight will be at the same spot. It is done so to keep your body familiar with the new environment and to escape from Altitude sickness.
Acclimatization Day will play a vital role to prevent you from Altitude sickness. This Day we will Hike up to Everest View Hotel which has an elevation of (3880m / 12729 ft.) It is also listed in the UNESCO World Heritage site.
You will encounter an amazing panoramic view of the Everest Region. Some of the popular Mountains are Mt. Lhotse, Mt Everest, Mt. Amadablam, Mt. Nuptse and Mt. Kongde, Mt. Thamserku and Mt. Khangtega and many more.
We will return back to Namche for lunch and after lunch, you will have a free day and can explore the local place.
Overnight at Namche Bazaar.
- Day 06: Namche - Tyangboche/ Diboche
This day after having breakfast we will head towards Debuche (3,820m). As we climb the Namche hill we will make a gentle slope walk for around an hour and then descend down to have the Lunch at Pungi Thanga which has an elevation of 3260m.
After having a delicious lunch after good Trek we will have to climb up for around an hour and reach a very magnificent and beautiful attraction called Tengboche. There lies one of the oldest and largest monasteries in the Everest Region. This location is the last point of Everest LuxuryTrek.
Overnight at Debuche
- Day 07: Tengboche to Monjo
Today we will start to descend back. After having our Breakfast we will start trekking towards Monjo. It will take around 6-7 hours of walk.
Overnight at Monjo
- Day 08: Monjo to Lukla
Today is the last Day of Trek. We will Trek down to Lukla. We will reach there by afternoon. You can enjoy the last day together with everyone.
Overnight at Lukla
- Day 09: Lukla to Kathmandu By Flight
Today we will fly from Lukla to Kathmandu. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Everest Luxury Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel. An extra free day is programmed by Sherpa Himalaya. A free day is kept to recover if in case the flight gets delayed.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free day as a day to explore and shop for your family, or else you can also make a short city tour.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 10: Free day In Kathmandu
As mentioned above this day is specially programmed to recover if the Lukla- Kathmandu flights get delayed due to uncertain change in weather.
With timely arrival, you can Explore the Thamel market and shop for your family. You can also make a short city tour to explore some World Heritage Site.
You will be invited for the Last farewell dinner with Sherpa Himalaya Executive.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 11: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-ta) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone and a certificate for your completion of Everest Luxury Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
