Dawa Rinji Sherpa
Trekking and Climbing GuideDawa Rinji Sherpa is a dedicated and experienced trekking and climbing guide who has devoted his life to exploring and sharing the majestic beauty of the Himalayas. With a deep-rooted passion for the mountains, he has been part of the tourism industry since childhood, steadily building a career that reflects his commitment, skill, and integrity.
Having reached the summits of some of the world’s most challenging peaks, including Mt. Everest, Mt. Makalu, and Mt. Lhotse, Dawa is a true mountaineering expert. His vast experience extends to numerous other high-altitude peaks such as Mera Peak, Island Peak, Yala Peak, Sribung Peak, Pisang Peak, Lobuche Peak, Singchuli Peak, and more, making him one of the most seasoned and reliable guides.
Dawa’s extensive knowledge of the Himalayan terrain and his dedication to ensuring the safety and satisfaction of every trekker and climber has played a key role in helping Sherpa Himalaya deliver exceptional experiences to its clients.
He holds various prestigious certifications, underscoring his proficiency and commitment to maintaining high safety standards and quality service:
- Trekking Guide Certification from the Government of Nepal (NATHM)
- Conservation and Biodiversity Training by the Trekking Agency Association of Nepal (TAAN)
- Mountaineering Course by the Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA)
- Basic Rock & Ice Climbing Training from the Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA)
Dawa Rinji Sherpa’s passion for the Himalayas, combined with his technical expertise and unwavering dedication to his clients, makes him an invaluable asset to Sherpa Himalaya. Whether you’re embarking on a challenging climb or a serene trek, Dawa’s wealth of experience guarantees an inspiring and safe adventure in the heart of the Himalayas.
