Ang Tsering Sherpa
Travel Consultant and GuideAng Tsering Sherpa is a dynamic and enthusiastic trekking and climbing guide whose passion for the Himalayas shines through in every adventure. Since the inception of Sherpa Himalaya Treks and Expedition, Ang has been an invaluable member of our team, leading clients on some of the most spectacular trails across Nepal.With an intimate knowledge of the region, he has not only trekked extensively across the Himalayas but has also climbed some of its iconic 6000-meter peaks, including Island Peak, Mera Peak, and Lobuche Peak. These personal accomplishments complement his expert guidance, helping trekkers from around the world experience the awe-inspiring beauty of the mountains.
Known for his friendly, approachable demeanor and meticulous care for clients, Ang goes above and beyond to ensure every trek and peak climb is a memorable experience. Clients consistently rave about the fun, laughter, and impromptu dances shared with Ang during their journeys—making every trek feel like a celebration in the mountains.
Ang’s qualifications reflect his commitment to professional growth and client safety. He holds certifications from some of the most respected institutions in Nepal, including:
- Trekking Guide Certification from the Government of Nepal (NATHM).
- Intensive Wilderness First Aid Training from Kathmandu Environmental Education Project (KEEP).
- Conservation and Biodiversity Training by the Trekking Agency Association of Nepal (TAAN).
- Mountaineering Course by the Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA).
- Basic Rock & Ice Climbing Training from the Nepal Mountaineering Association (NMA).
Fluent in English and a talented photographer, Ang adds an extra layer of value to his guiding services. His keen eye for stunning landscapes ensures that clients leave with not only memories but beautiful photos to treasure. Whether you’re trekking to Everest Base Camp or climbing a remote peak, Ang’s expertise, positive energy, and warm personality will make your Himalayan adventure truly unforgettable.
