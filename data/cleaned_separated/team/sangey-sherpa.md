Sangey Sherpa
Trekking GuideSangey Sherpa is a strong, dedicated young guide with a natural affinity for the mountains. Born into a family of accomplished mountaineers, Sangey inherited his deep connection to the Himalayas from his father, an experienced mountain guide. Growing up surrounded by the wonders of the trekking and mountaineering world, Sangey developed an early understanding of the tourism industry, which has been pivotal in shaping his successful career.
Since joining Sherpa Himalaya in 2020, Sangey has quickly demonstrated his leadership qualities, exceptional skills, and a deep passion for helping others experience the breathtaking beauty of Nepal’s mountains. His commitment to teamwork, respect for nature, and care for clients has made him a valued member of our team. Sangey believes in creating a positive and harmonious atmosphere, ensuring that every trekker feels both safe and inspired throughout their journey.
Currently working as a trekking guide, Sangey has a comprehensive knowledge of Nepal’s most renowned trekking routes, from the Everest region to Annapurna, Langtang, and beyond. His ultimate goal is to further his expertise and one day become a mountain climbing expert, offering clients the opportunity to challenge themselves on some of the world’s highest peaks.
Whether you are trekking through the lush valleys of the Annapurna region or venturing to the foot of the mighty Everest, Sangey Sherpa is ready to guide you through Nepal’s most beautiful and challenging trails. With his deep-rooted love for the Himalayas and unwavering dedication to client satisfaction, you can trust Sangey to lead you on an unforgettable adventure, filled with awe, discovery, and respect for the mountains.
