Darinje Sherpa
Trekking and Climbing GuideDarinje Sherpa is a dynamic and highly skilled climbing guide with over 10 years of experience in the trekking and mountaineering industry. A valuable member of the Sherpa Himalaya team, Darinje’s primary focus is on guiding in the challenging and awe-inspiring mountains of Nepal. His journey to becoming one of the leading climbing guides in the Himalayas began humbly as a porter, but through dedication and a relentless passion for the mountains, he has transformed into one of the region’s most accomplished guides.
Darinje has successfully summited numerous prestigious peaks, including several of the world’s most famous 8000-meter mountains, such as Mt. Everest, Mt. Annapurna, Mt. Dhaulagiri, Mt. Manaslu, Mt. Amadablam, and Mt. Himlung. His vast experience also extends to several other notable peaks, including Pisang Peak, Larke Peak, Chulu Far East, Chulu West Peak, Mera Peak, Island Peak, Lobuche Peak, and countless others.
Darinje’s extensive mountaineering expertise, combined with his deep passion for the tourism industry, allows Sherpa Himalaya Treks and Expedition to provide unparalleled service to clients from all over the world. His professionalism, skill, and commitment to safety have earned him a reputation as a trusted guide in the Himalayas.
Whether you’re trekking in the valleys or climbing some of the world’s highest peaks, Darinje Sherpa’s knowledge, experience, and dedication ensure that every client enjoys a safe and rewarding Himalayan adventure.
