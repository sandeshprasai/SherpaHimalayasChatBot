Pokhara lies 200 km west of Kathmandu City (Capital). It is the second-largest metropolitan city in the Gandaki zone. Pokhara City has an elevation of 830m in the southern part.
Pokhara city has the ability to provide one of the most relax and beautiful trips that can be topped with amazing adventuring activities. You can enjoy one of the most beautiful lake known as Fewa Taal.
Fewa Taal is a freshwater lake to the southern part of the city. It is also the second-largest lake in Nepal. You can have amazing boating on it. If you are lucky enough you can have breathtaking sunset view and reflection of Annapurna range mountains.
Similarly, Sarankot (1600m) is one of the major attractions for the Pokhara City Tour. It is a maximum a hour drive from the city. You will be taken to the nearby hill located on the Western side. It is the same hill where Paragliding is performed.
You can have a stunning view of Sunset as well as the sunrise view over Annapurna range Himalayas. Mountains such as Dhaulagiri(8167m), Annapurna range, Manaslu (8163m), and one of the most beautiful known mountain called Mt. Fishtail (Macchapucchare)-6993m can be witnessed.
One of the major adventures you can make in Pokhara City is Paragliding. On your request, you can conquer this amazing adventure. You will be taken to Sarankot Hill for the elevation.
All the above mentioned mountains can be seen amazingly and can be your lifetime memory over Fewa Lake and Pokhara City. your flight will be around 15 minutes and you will be taken to the hotel back.
Hence, with the combination of adventures such as Bungee, Zipline, and Paragliding with beautiful attractions such as Few Lake, Sarankot, and Beautiful panoramic view of Himalaya most of the travelers who have limited time here in Nepal make the decision for one of the tours: Pokhara City Tour.
