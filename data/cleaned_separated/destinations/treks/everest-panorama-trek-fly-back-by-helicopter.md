Everest View Short Trek is one of the best and most demanding package of Sherpa Himalaya. In this amazing trek you will make a climb of around 640 m altitude starting from Lukla (2800m).
Your last trek point will be Namche Bazzar but after a Day stop in Namche Bazaar for acclimatization you will be taken to a place which has been a life time dream of 100’s.
This Trek is specially programmed for the travelers who have limited time along with who wants to get introduced to the amazing trail of Everest Region.
In this Everest View Trek with Fly back by Helicopter you will first introduced to adventurous plane flight from Kathmandu to Lukla (2800m). Lukla is also known as “Gateway of Everest Region”. This short flight will be the start of your Adventure in Himalaya. If you are lucky enough to get a flight on a good weather day you will witness Himalayan Ranges clearly with amazing Landscape of Nepal.
As you land to Lukla and have breakfast/lunch depending upon time you reach there your real trek starts. On the very first day you will not have difficult trek. You will trek for 3-4 hours this Day.
Similarly, the following day will be more challenging the first Day. You will Trek to Namche Bazaar. Namche Bazaar is one of the attractive location on the Trail of Everest Region. On the day of acclimatization you will view the World Highes Mountain Mt. Everest-8848m from Namche View Point (chyorkim) or from Shyangboche.
Following Day, You will be picked by Helicopter and you will have breathtaking mountain flight and will be landed at Kalapathar. You will spend some time there mesmerizing the breathtaking mighty Mountains and then eventually, we will fly back to Lukla on same Helicopter and fly back to Kathmandu via Aeroplane

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival In Kathmandu
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and explore around. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around.
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Everest View Short Trek. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya
Overnight at KTM
- Day 02: Trek PreParation day in kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Everest Base Camp Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in the airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Kathmandu to Lukla By Flight and Trek to Phakding
Today you will need to wake up early in the morning. You will be notified by Sherpa Himalaya about the time. You will be received by a private vehicle and dropped to Airport together with the Guide. You will have a very amazing Flight to Lukla from Kathmandu. You will witness Lovely villages and Aerial view of Himalayan ranges around. With clear weather, you will have a clear idea about the Landscape of the Hilly and Himalayan region.
As you arrive Lukla, we will take a short Rest and have breakfast or lunch according to the time of Arrival. After that, you will start your Everest View Short Trek trail to Phakdhing. It will take around 2-3 Hours of Trek. You will make your overnight stay here.
Phakdhing is Elevated at 2610m/ 8562 ft. Before you arrive at Phakdhing the trail will be normal with some steep downhill and with normal uphill Trek. You will also have the first sight of Mountain Kusum Khangguru (6,369m) from Thado Koshi which is at height of 2580m.
Overnight at Phakdhing
- Day 04: Trek from Phakding to Namche Bazzar
After a very comfortable stay at Phakdhing, we will climb up towards Namche Bazaar (3440 m/11,280 feet), Namche Bazar is a small town in Himalaya which used to be very famous trade center.
You will encounter an amazing panoramic view of the Everest Region. Some of the popular Mountains are Mt. Lhotse, Mt Everest, Mt. Amadablam, Mt. Nuptse and Mt. Kongde, Mt. Thamserku and Mt. Khangtega and many more. Today we will cross over the Hillary Bridge which is also one of the adventurous tasks to make. As we cross the Bridge we will hike straight uphill for almost 2 t0 3 hours to reach the overnight stop(Namche Bazaar). Overnight at Namche Bazaar
- Day 05: Day Hiking in Namche Bazzar
Acclimatization is an Extra Day where you will be introduced to a new elevation but the overnight will be at the same spot. It is done so to keep your body familiar with the new environment and to escape from Altitude sickness.
Acclimatization Day will play a vital role to prevent you from Altitude sickness. This Day we will Hike up to Everest View Hotel which has an elevation of (3880m / 12729 ft.) It is also listed in the UNESCO World Heritage site.
You will encounter an amazing panoramic view of the Everest Region. Some of the popular Mountains are Mt. Lhotse, Mt Everest, Mt. Amadablam, Mt. Nuptse and Mt. Kongde, etc and many more.

Overnight at Namche Bazaar
- Day 06: Early Morning Moutain Flight to Kalapathar and Back To Lukla
Today after breakfast you will be picked by a Helicopter. You will have an amazing flight to Kalapathar. The flight will pass between the ridges and over the trail of Everest Base Camp. You can witness small villages below and also the amazing landscapes of the mountains of the Everest Region.
As we reach Kalapathar, we will have very limited time there so, we will enjoy the beautiful moment there and capture the landscape around.
Then soon later we will fly back to Lukla. Today will be the last day of the short trek.
Overnight at Lukla
- Day 07: Lukla to Kathmandu Flight
Today we will fly from Lukla to Kathmandu. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Three Pass Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 08: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Everest Base Camp Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.

Additional Information
Due to recent decision made by Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA), there is no direct flight from Kathmandu to Lukla. So, you will have to make a 4-5 hours long drive to Ramechap from Kathmandu and then the next morning you will have flight to Lukla which will be 15 minute long.
Ramechap is a small village untouched by modern facility so the accommodation there will be a normal hotel.
