Shortest Annapurna Base Camp Trek – 10 Days, Annapurna base camp trek is an amazing walk...
Tilicho Lake Mesokanto Pass Trek is the most remarkable and awesome journey where Sherpa Himalaya Treks & Expedition and its expert guides will lead you to enjoy this wonderful and exciting adventure.
Tilicho Lake Mesokanta Pass Trekking is located in the high scenic Manang Valley with the incredible beauty of the Western Himalayan region. This marvelous trek leads on the popular and famous Annapurna circuit trail for a week and then heads into a remote isolated corner of Manang around beautiful Tilicho Lake, ending this amazing adventure at Mustang area in Jomsom after crossing scenic high Mesokanta pass.
Tilicho Lake Mesokanto Pass Trek begins with an overland drive from Kathmandu to warm areas of the Lamjung district at Syange village by the raging Marshyangdi River.
From here our scenic walk starts heading higher towards beautiful Manang Valley, trek leads past many lovely traditional villages of Dharapani to Chame headquarters town of Manang district.
After Chame air gets cooler as our walk leads closer to the mountains of northern Annapurna Himalaya, from here on gain high altitude as the trek head towards dry and barren landscapes after leaving the forested area.
The walk is on the main trail of Annapurna Circuit to Manang village, this trekking area is listed as world’s top ten destinations by travel magazines and guide books. On reaching Manang village surrounded by spectacular snow peaks a tour of Braga’s old and important monastery with ancient artifacts and monuments.
In Manang rest and acclimatization day with time to explore villages of Tibetan Buddhism culture in the great backdrop of Gangapurna-Annapurna II and III.
From Manang villages, our adventure diverts to Tilicho Lake with its mind-blowing scenery of mountains, enjoyable time in Tilicho with its emerald and turquoise color lake, and then our adventure continues to cross above the highest point of this trip over Mesokanta-la pass at 5,099 m / 16,729 ft.
After a wonderful moment on top with glorious views downhill to Mustang region at Jomsom town (headquarter town of Mustang district) conclude this gorgeous adventure Tilicho Lake Mesokanta-la pass trekking.
From Jomsom a short scenic flight to picturesque Pokhara city, a famous and popular touristic spot in the Himalaya region, located beneath the massive Annapurna Mountains with its pristine lakes Phewa and Begnas.
Trekking with Sherpa Himalaya Treks & Expedition Pvt.Ltd.will be enjoyable with exciting times as our guides are well equipped with vast knowledge to offer you of the area of the trek, from culture-geography-history-flora and fauna including medical know-how if needed.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: arrival in Kathmandu
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Drive from Kathmandu to Besi Sahar and local jeep to Dhara Pani (1960m)
Today we will have a long day drive. As we finish our breakfast we will head towards Dharapani via Besisahar. It will take 6 Hours to drive to besisahar by private vehicle. After short rest we will again have to drive 2 hours long offroad to reah afternoon camp Dharapani.
This drive will be very interesting and perfect time to know more about the landscape of Nepal.
Overnight at Dharapani
- Day 03: Trek From Dharapani to Chame (2670m)
Today is our first Trek day. We will start our Trek from Dharapani, heading towards Chame which is elevated at altitude of 2710m.
Your trek will be 5-6 hours long along the river side enjoying beautiful nature and mountain such as Mt. Lamjung and Mt. Annapurna
Overnight at Chame
- Day 04: Trek From Chame to Pisang (3200m)
After we finish our breakfast we will head towards Pisang. The trek will be normal with slight slopes. It will take Normally 5 hours to reach Pisang.
You will find two Pisang; Upper Pisang and Lower Pisang. The camp site will be decided by then while we get close to. Both the Pisang are nearby so, it is similar wherever we stay for camp. You can witness Mt. Annapurna II directly from Pisang
Overnight at Pisang
- Day 05: Trek from Pisang to Ngawal (3700m/12139 ft) 4-5 hours
Today you will have a normal trek with an almost 2-hour uphill climb. As you complete the uphill climb you will be introduced to one of the oldest and traditional villages of the Annapurna Region; well known as Gyaru.
After a short break at Gyaru with a furthermore, 1 and half hour normal trek you will reach Ngawal. One of the best locations providing you breathtaking views of Mt. Annapurna II, IV, II, Mt. Gangapurna, Mt. Tilicho, Mt. Chulu East, Far East, and Mt. Pisang.
Overnight at Ngawal
- Day 06: Trek from Ngawal to Manang (3,500m/11,482feet), 3-4 hours
Today we will reach a beautiful small village called Manang. Manang is also the perfect location to acclimatize your body to altitude. Today you will have a normal descend and sideways trek.
With normal 3-4 hours of Trek, we will reach Manang.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 07: Acclimatazation day
Today is the acclimatization day. This day is specially kept to let your body acclimatize with the altitude and the environment. You can take this day as a rest day too.
This day is also known as hike up sleep low. Today we will hike to Chongur View Point crossing through Gangapurna Glacier. you will also encounter beautiful glacier lake.
After we finish our exploration around we will come back to camp and be prepared for the next day.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 08: Trek From Manang to Shree Kharka(3850m)
Manang is also a location which separate two trails; one to Tilicho lake and another towards Thorongla Pass directly.
Since we will visit the Tilicho lake in the beginning we will head towards Shree Kharka. Today we will need 5-6 hours of trek.
Overnight at Shree Kharka
- Day 09: Trek from Shree Kharka to Tilicho Base camp (4150m)
As we finish our breakfast we will be heading towards Tilicho Base Camp. Today our trek will be a normal 4-5 hours long. The trails are narrow and slight slope.
Overnight at Tilicho Base Camp
- Day 10: Trek from Base Camp to Tilicho lake (4990m)
Today is the day to reach our first destination; Tilicho Lake. We will head early morning to Tilicho lake.
We will explore Tilicho lake take beautiful pictures of the lake and come back to base camp.
Overnight at Tilicho Lake
- Day 11: Trek from Tilicho lake to Chauri kharka/ Yak kharka via Mesokanta Pass
Today as we will pass over Mesokanta Pass. We will climb steep uphill and give some time to enjoy the view from pass and also capture some beautiful moments from the pass.
After the pass we will descend down and reach Yak Kharka.
Overnight at Yak Kharka
- Day 12: Trek from Yak kharka to Jomsom
Today our destination will be Jomsom. The trail is most of the time down descend with normal slopes. It will take 4-5 hours to reach Jomsom.
Overnight at Jomsom
- Day 13: Fly back from Jomsom to Pokhara
As we finish our Breakfast today we will have direct flight from Jomsom to Pokhara.
Our flight will be early in the morning so, if you can freshen yourself as you reach pokhara and explore around.
- Day 14: Pokhara to kathmandu Flight or Drive
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Mesokanto Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free time to explore and shop for your family or else you can also make short city tour.
- Day 15: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Mesokanto Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
