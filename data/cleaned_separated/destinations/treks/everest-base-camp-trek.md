Everest Base Camp Trek (5364m/17598 ft) is one of the most adventurous and most amazing journeys in the Himalayas of Nepal. On this Day, Everest Base Camp has become the goal of hundreds of Trekkers. It has stood as an achievement, Encouragement, and also as a motivation to all the world.
It is sure that Everest Base Camp Trek will make take you very close to the unexplainably beautiful nature and also to an altitude where you will feel yourself completely above all.
Mt. Everest 8848m/29029 ft Lies in Solukhumbu District which lies in the Eastern Development Region of Nepal. Going Deeper into the Development Region this iconic destination lies in Khumbu Region which is also a border to Neighboring country Tibet(China). This amazing Destination is also a core of Sagarmatha National Park which almost covers 30% of Solukhumbhu District.
Everest Base Camp Trek has been able to provide travelers a clear view of 4 out of 6 tallest mountains of the world. They are; Mt. Everest (8848m / 29029 ft), Mt. Makalu (8470m/27789 ft), Mt. Lhotse (8516 m/27940 ft) and Mt. Cho Oyu (8201m/26906 ft)
Everest Base Camp Trek trails reflect the footstep of Tenzing Norgay Sherpa and Sir Edmund Hillary who were the first person to make the summit of Mt. Everest in 1953 AD. Due to the proper facility of Hotels and Lodge this region is more popular with Tea House Trek.
Your Trek will start by making an approx. A 30-minute flight from Kathmandu to Lukla(2800m/9186 ft). On this short flight, you will witness an aerial view of almost all the mountain ranges. If you are lucky enough to get your first flight in the morning then you are likely to experience breathtaking sunrise.
The trail slowly heads towards Namche Bazaar 3340m/11,290 ft. You will have a day off at Namche Bazaar for acclimatization of your body. You will make a short hike around Namche Bazaar and have overnight at the same altitude.
Similarly, you will ascend higher altitude each day with proper altitude differences. With similar after continuous ascend of around a week, you will reach another acclimating stop point which is Dingboche 4410m/14468ft. Until you arrive at this beautiful stop you will cover almost 65% of your last destination; Everest Base Camp.
On Day 10th from your start of Trekking, you will reach to the Final Destination. This Day will be your lifetime experience and a big achievement. After enjoying your precious time in Everest Base Camp you will have overnight at Gorakshep and Return back from the same trail after making a hike to Kalapathar.
Everest Region is Highly populated by Buddhist People and it is also known as the “homeland of Sherpa’s “. You will find many small Stupa (Chorten) with flags having mixed colors, some monasteries on the trail; A big monastery is situated at an elevation of 3,860 m (12,660 ft) built-in 1923 AD. It is one of the most Devoted monasteries by the Buddhist People of Nepal.
During Everest Base Camp Trek your stay will be at the hotel where you can choose your meals as per the menu provided by the hotel. The highest overnight stay will be at an elevation of 5164m/16942 ft which is of Gorakshep. The Everest Base is 2-2.5 hours of Hike from Gorakshep.
On the day of Return from Gorakshep, you can make an early morning Hike to Kalapathar (5545m/12192 ft) which will be the Highest Elevation of the Everest Base Camp Trek. This hike to Kalapathar will also take around 2 to 2.5 hours giving you a breathtaking panoramic view of the Everest Region mountains.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival day in Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft)
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trek Pre paration Day in kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Everest Base Camp Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Flight from Kathmandu – Lukla and Trek to Phakding (2,652m/8,700 ft), 3 – 4 hours
Today you will need to wake up early in the morning. You will be notified by Sherpa Himalaya about the time. You will be received by a private vehicle and dropped to Airport together with the Guide. You will have a very amazing Flight to Lukla from Kathmandu. You will witness very beautiful villages and Aerial view of Himalayan ranges around. With clear weather, you will have a clear idea about the Landscape of the Hilly and Himalayan region.
As you arrive Lukla, we will take a short Rest and have breakfast or lunch according to the time of Arrival. After that, you will start your Everest Base Camp Trek trail to Phakdhing. It will take around 2-3 Hours of Trek. You will make your overnight stay here.
Phakdhing is Elevated at 2610m/ 8562 ft. Before you arrive at Phakdhing the trail will be normal with some steep downhill and with normal uphill Trek. You will also have the first sight of Mountain Kusum Khangguru (6,369m) from Thado Koshi which is at height of 2580m.
Overnight at Phakdhing
- Day 04: Trek from Phakding – Namche Bazar (3,440 m/11,280 ft), 5 – 6 hours
After a very comfortable stay at Phakdhing, we will climb up towards Namche Bazaar (3440 m/11,280 feet), Namche Bazar is a small town in Himalaya which used to be very famous trade center.
Today we will cross over the Hillary Bridge which is also one of the adventurous task to make. As we cross the Bridge we will hike straight uphill for almost 2 t0 3 hours to reach the overnight stop(Namche Bazaar). People used to get things from Tibet and sell making the main market point Namche. You will have plenty of hotels and souvenir shops. You can also get an option to buy any Trekking gears and necessary clothes you might need in case you missed any from Kathmandu or Luka.
During your ascent to Namche Bazar, you will encounter 5 Bridges to cross. This moment will also be one of the memorable. This Day will be a bit harder than the previous Day. You will face a normal uphill walk as well as steep uphill. With recent updates, the trails are made more proper and easier.
Overnight at Namche Bazaar
- Day 05: Acclimatization Day – Namche Bazaar, 3,440m/11,284 ft
Acclimatization is an Extra Day where you will be introduced to a new elevation but the overnight will be at the same spot. It is done so to keep your body familiar with the new environment and to escape from Altitude sickness.
Acclimatization Day will play a vital role to prevent you from Altitude sickness. This Day we will Hike up to Everest View Hotel which has an elevation of (3880m / 12729 ft.) It is also listed in the UNESCO World Heritage site.
You will encounter an amazing panoramic view of the Everest Region. Some of the popular Mountains are Mt. Lhotse, Mt Everest, Mt. Amadablam, Mt. Nuptse and Mt. Kongde, Mt. Thamserku and Mt. Khangtega and many more.
We will return back to Namche for lunch and after lunch, you will have a free day and can explore the local place.
Overnight at Namche Bazaar
- Day 06: Trek from Namche Bazaar to Tengboche (3,860m/12533 ft), 6 – 7 hours walk
This day after having breakfast we will head towards Tengboche (3,860m). As we climb the Namche hill we will make a gentle slope walk for around an hour and then descend down to have the Lunch at Pungi Thanga which has an elevation of 3260m.
After having a delicious lunch after good Trek we will have to climb up for around an hour and reach a very magnificent and beautiful attraction called Tengboche. There lies one of the oldest and largest Monasteries in Everest Region. This location is also the last point of Everest Panorama Trek.
This Destination can be for the ones who are not much well prepared or lack physical fitness for the Everest Base Camp Trek.
Overnight at Tengboche
- Day 07: Trek from Tengboche to Dingboche (4,410 m/ 14469 ft), 5 – 6 hours
Now we have already reached the half trail of Everest Base Camp Trek. Now you are at higher altitude the surrounding environment and plant density gets thinner. You will witness less tall trees with small bushes of herbs and Shrubs. This Day our destination will be Dhingboche (4,410m). We will have a normal uphill Trek. You will encounter small villages and some small monasteries. We will be trekking along the riverside enjoying the beautiful and peaceful nature.
Overnight at Dhingboche
- Day 08: Acclimatized day in Dingboche.
Today is a rest day here in Dingboche. You can explore around and hike to nearby viewpoints and come back overnight. People say this day as Trek high and sleep low day.
As earlier in Namche Bazar this day is this day you can enjoy your hike or rest in the hotel.
Overnight at Dingboche
- Day 09: Trek from Dingboche to Lobuche (4910m) 5– 6 hours
As similar to other days after breakfast we will Trek towards Lobuche (4,910 m). This Day we will have gradual upslope with normal Trail until you reach Thukla 4620m. As we reach Thukla we will have a steep climb upward and reach Lobuche.
This Day you will also be introduced to a memorial spot of Climbers and Sherpa who passed away on Everest Expedition. You will also have sight of some of the beautiful mountains like Lobuche Peak, Mt. Pumori, Mt. Cholatse, Mt. Tobuche and Mt. Chhumbu.
Now you have reached very close to your final Destination Everest Base Camp Trek. The Next day you will be at your last Destination “Everest Base Camp”.
Overnight at Lobuche
- Day 10: Trek from Lobuche to Gorak Shep [5170 m]-Everest Base Camp [5364 m]-Gorak Shep, 7 –8 hours
Today is the day that you will reach your Final Destination Everest Base Camp. After early breakfast, we will climb towards Gorak Shep. After a few hours of an uphill climb, we will reach Gorak Shep ( 5164 m).
We will have lunch at Gorak Shep and then Make our Everest Base Camp Trek successful by making a Trek for 2 hours to reach the base of Everest (5364 m). As we head towards the final base camp you will have sight of Everest Base Camp, Khumbu Glacier, Mt. Lingtren, Mt, Khumbutse, Mt. Changtse and Mt. Nuptse.
We will spend some time at the base camp and share this beautiful moment together, Capture our memories which will be proof of our arrival. Similarly, we will return back to Gorakshep and overnight stay here.
Overnight at Gorakshep
- Day 11: Trek from Gorak Shep – Kala Patthar – Pheriche [4280 m/14,070 feet], 8 – 9 hours
This morning we will hike to Kala Pathar (5550m) to see the scenic view of the Everest Region mountains. You will have a beautiful sunrise view on Mt. Everest. This hike is kept as optional.
If we plan to go there, we will go early morning without having breakfast and come back to Gorakshep to have breakfast. It will take 2-2.5 hours to reach there and then Return back slowly from the same trail and stay at Pheriche.
Overnight at Pheriche
- Day 12: Trek from Pheriche-Pangboche-Tengboche-Namche Bazaar (3,440 m/11,280 feet), 6 – 7 hours
As usual, we will Trek down to Namche Bazaar via Tengboche. The trail is the same which we climbed up.
Overnight at Namche Bazaar
- Day 13: Trek from Namche Bazaar-Lukla (2,800m/9,186feet): 6 – 7 hours
Trekking down from the same trail we made earlier, we are at Lukla. The trail down will be easier than earlier ascends.
Today is the last day of your Everest Base Camp Trek and meet with the staff. After the last day meet and greet with the members we will prepare for our flight to Kathmandu tomorrow.
Overnight at Lukla
- Day 14: Flight from Lukla –Kathmandu.
Today we will fly from Lukla to Kathmandu. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Everest Base camp, you can have a proper rest at your hotel. An extra free day is programmed by Sherpa Himalaya. A free day is kept to recover if in case the flight gets delayed.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free day as a day to explore and shop for your family or else you can also make a short city tour.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 15: Free Day in Kathmandu
As mentioned above this day is specially programmed to recover if the Lukla- Kathmandu flights get delayed due to uncertain change in weather.
With timely arrival, you can Explore the Thamel market and shop for your family. You can also make a short city tour to explore some World Heritage Site.
You will be invited for the Last farewell dinner with Sherpa Himalaya Executive.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 16: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and a certificate for your completion of Everest Base Camp Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
