Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu and Transfer to Hote
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trek Preparation in Kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Tashi Lapcha Pass Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and the rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Drive from Kathmandu to Gongor Khola (1,440m/4,726ft) - 8 to 9 hours drive
Early in the morning, we will drive off to Gongor Khola, Dolakha. For an initial couple of hours, we will follow the Arniko Highway with the Bhotekoshi and Sunkoshi rivers flowing down on the side. You might witness people rafting in the white waters of the thrilling rivers. The road from thereon is zig zag, and at some places, it is not blacktopped. Upon entering Dolaha, you will drive further up to Charikot and continue on the rugged road to Gangor Khola. The village of Gangor is located near the riverbank of the same name. It lies in the shadow of the Tamakoshi hydroelectric dam project. There is a big construction site.
- Day 04: Trek to Simi Gaon (2,000m)
We begin the initial leg of the trek by taking the north road to Chetchet. The trail sees a couple of settlements in between on the right side. There is a suspension bridge over the Tamakoshi River, which you’ll be crossing to take an ascent on the steep rocky slope. We hike up to a forested area from thereon then cross a series of terraced fields in Simigaun. The village is green and spreads over the side of the hill. It is inhabited by a mix of different cultures and traditions, from the ethnic Sherpa to Chhetris. As it is a relatively short trek, you can lay down your backpacks, after some rest, head to the village, and interact with the warm and welcoming locals.
- Day 05: Trek from Simigaon to Dong Dhing( 2,791 m)
Today your trek starts after breakfast. Your trail passes into rhododendron forest which leads you toward Rolwaling Valley. Until you reach a place called Gyalche you will have a hard climb then you descend down towards Rolwaling Khola. Today your trek will be 6-7 hours long.
Overnight at Dong Dhing.
- Day 06: Trek from Dong Dhing to Bedhing ( 3,740 m)
Today your destination will be a small Sherpa village known as Bedhing. Most of the youngsters work in the mountaineering field as climbers of this village. You will also encounter a small historic monastery in the village.
Today your trek will be around 6 hours with a normal uphill climb and challenging uphill in between. Today you will also have a view of Mt Melungtse 7181m.
Overnight at Bedhing
- Day 07: Trek from Bedhing to Nagau ( 4,200 m)
As you finish your breakfast you will head to a beautiful village just below the Tso Rolpa Glacier Lake. A valley that offers you a view of Mt. Chobutse 6685 m, Mt. Yalung Ri 5630m, and Mt, Chugimago 6259m.
Today your trek will be 5-6 hours long through thin juniper and rhododendron forest. The vegetation starts decreasing with the increase in elevation. This is also s popular rest camp.
Overnight at Na
- Day 08: Acclimatization day at Nagau
Acclimatization is an Extra Day where you will be introduced to a new elevation but the overnight will be at the same spot. It is done so to keep your body familiar with the new environment and to escape from Altitude sickness.
Acclimatization Day will play a vital role to prevent you from Altitude sickness. This day you will hike to Yalung La Pass to an elevation of 4900m. you will get an amazing panoramic view of the mountains of the region. Ramdung Peak 5930m and Yalung Ri 55630m can be seen very closely with Tso Rolpa Glacier.
After a quality time at the top of the ridge, you descend down to Na for overnight camp.
Overnight at Na
- Day 09: Trek from Nagau to chugima ( 4,920 m) Tent Camp
Today you climb towards the southern side of the Tsho Rolpa Lake climbing across the natural dam that saves the whole valley. Nothern side trails are just printed on old maps which today does not exist.
Today you will have a harder climb crossing moraine and rocky trail. Your trek will be 5-6 hours long today.
Overnight at Chugima (tent)
- Day 10: Trek from Chugima to Jaboo Glacier (Tolumbau)-5205m
Today will be a tough and technical day. You will have to pass through a glacier and rocky spurs where setting fixed ropes and putting crampons can also be necessary. Trakarding glacier is a major obstacle during today’s trail. It is a huge iced rock that shines bluish. You will witness glacial streams, falling ice, and rocks during the pass which will favor and make the day more adventurous.
Overnight at Tolumbau (tent)
- Day 11: Trek from Tolambau to Thengba via Tashi Lapcha Pass(5755m)
Today your camp will be just close to Tashi Lapcha Pass. Your trail will be through snow and over the glacier. Your climb offers you a beautiful view of Mt. Dragkar, Mt.Bigphereo, Mt. Tengi Ragi, and many peaks of Nepal including several from the Tibet side. After around 7-8 hours you will reach Tashi Phuk.
Today you will climb the Tashi Lapcha Pass 5755m one of the most challenging passes and also the pass which connects Rolwaling to the Everest Region. Today you will camp around 300m below crossing Tashi Lapcha Pass or if you get extra time you can trek down to Thengba.
Overnight at Thengba (tent/tea house)
- Day 12: Trek from Thengba to Thame (3800m) 5-6 hours walk
As usual, your trek starts after you finish your breakfast. The trails are much easier and you will have more descend through small Sherpa villages and monasteries. Thame used to be a famous trading market in ancient days with Tibetans. Your Trek will be around 6 hours long.
Overnight at Thame
- Day 13: Trek from Thame to Namche Bazzar (3440 m )
An easy day to trek in Khumbu Region. Today you will have a short trek to one of the beautiful attractive villages in the Everest Region; Namche Bazaar. This town is also one of the most developed and has a facility of almost everything from the high-quality hotel, bars, and shop.
Overnight at Namche Bazaar
- Day 14: Trek from Namche Bazaar to Lukla (2860m)
Your trail goes downhill for 5-6 hours until you reach the base of Lukla. With an hour gentle uphill climb you arrive at Lukla.
Today is the last day of your Tashi Lapcha Pass Trek and meet with the staff. After the last day meets and greets with the members we will prepare for our flight to Kathmandu tomorrow.
Overnight at Lukla
- Day 15: Flight from Lukla to Kathmandu
Today we will fly from Lukla to Kathmandu. You will be picked by the Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped at Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Tashi Lapcha Pass Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel. An extra free day is programmed by Sherpa Himalaya. A free day is kept to recover if in case the flight gets delayed.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free day as a day to explore and shop for your family, or else you can also make a short city tour.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 16: Free Day in Kathmandu
As mentioned above this day is specially programmed to recover if the Lukla- Kathmandu flights get delayed due to uncertain changes in weather.
With timely arrival, you can Explore the Thamel market and shop for your family. You can also make a short city tour to explore some World Heritage Site.
You will be invited for the Last farewell dinner with Sherpa Himalaya Executive.
Overnight at KTM
- Day 17: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
