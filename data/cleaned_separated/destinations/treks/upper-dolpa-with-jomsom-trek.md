Upper Dolpa with Jomsom Trek start as we pass through many ancient monasteries and highland villages and wonderful natural scenery as we make our way to Phoksundo Lake and the fabled Shey Gompa. We explore alpine meadows and exotic villages in Upper Dolpo which has kept Bhoriya culture alive. We also get to experience Thakkali culture in Mustang.
The most interesting factor of this Upper Dolpa with Jomsom Trek is that we get to witness the cultural difference of the people of different regions, had centuries ago. Dolpo the high mountainous settlement holds the nomads that migrated to the region ages ago. Similarly the people of Mustang also called Lopas also migrated from Tibet almost during the same time. Still the contrast between the people of these two regions in the past and in the recent times is so huge in terms of civilization; it is an interesting discovery.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu/ (1400 m)
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trek Preparation Day
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Upper Dolpa with Jomsom Trek. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Kathmandu to Nepalgunj (150m)/ flight
Today as you finish your breakfast you will be picked by your guide in the hotel and head towards TIA airport and make a scenic flight to Nepalgunj.
Overnight at Nepalgunj
- Day 04: Nepalgunj to Juphal (2475m)/ flight and Juphal to Dunai/ 3 hrs trek.
Today again we will head to Nepalgunj airport after we finish our breakfast. We will have an amazing flight to Juphal.
You will be witness beautiful monasteries, river and landscape from your flight. after around 35 minutes of flight, you will be at Juphal. After a short trek, you will reach a small and beautiful town Dunai.
Overnight at Dunai
- Day 05: Dunai to Chhepka (2687m)/ 6 hrs
As we finish our breakfast we will slowly head to the trail of Dolpa. Our trail; will be across a small village called Sulighar. We will have an average of 5 hours of Trek today. The trail is a beautiful and comfortable path.
After finishing all the documentation we will enter into She Phoksundo National Park.
Now we trek into isolated small villages full of green vegetation and also a suspension bridge. This Trek is one of the perfect treks to exchange culture and tradition.
Overnight at Chhepka
- Day 06: Chhepka to Chunuwar (3110m)/ 6 hrs
As we finish the breakfast, we will head towards our destination Chunuwar. The trail is similar to the previous day. Today we will inter into Lower Dolpo Area.
Today we will cross over Phoksundo Lake through an adventurous suspension bridge. As we cross some small villages Kharka and Rechi and reach our night destination Chunuwar.
Overnight at Chunuwar
- Day 07: Chunuwar to Phoksundo Lake (3600m)/ 5-6 hrs
Today we will reach one of the best destination; Phoksundo Lake. We will be passing through small chaityas and small houses. We will trek 4-5 hours normal uphill Trek
As you reach the Lake you rich the lake you will get relief from all your tiredness you faced all days long.
Overnight at Phoksundo
- Day 08: Phoksundo – Rest day
Today we will explore the lake and also visit a small village known as Ringmo Village. There is also a very old monastery which is known as Tshowa Monastery.
This monastery is at the ridge top above the amazing Phoksundo lake. As we make our short hike to the top we will slowly return back to camp.
Overnight at Phoksundo Lake.
- Day 09: Phoksundo- Sallaghari/ 5-6hrs trek
We will have our breakfast and start to Trek to reach today’s destination Sallaghari. We will go by the cliffs, a thin fine forest, and some small houses.
Today will be a long day Trek which will be around 6-7 hours.
Overnight at Sallaghari
- Day 10: Sallaghari to Nangdala HC (4717 m)/ 5-6 hrs trek
Today our destination will be Nandala High Camp. The high camp elevation is 4100m. The trail will lead you next side of Kyaksa River.
Going over a suspension bridge and few hike forward you will arrive at Nangdala High Camp.
Overnight at Nangdala High Camp
- Day 11: Nangdala HC to Shey Gompa/ ( 4280m) Crossing Nangdala pass (5240m)/ 5-6hrs
As usual, we will head towards the destination after finishing breakfast. Today our stop well be at one of the most beautiful places in Dolpa.
We will keep heading for Nangda-la (In Tibetan Language La means to Ridge). From the ridge, you will get one of the best views over around landscape, Vegetation, Chorten, and. some peaks.
After crossing the ridge we will overstay at Shey Gompa.
Overnight at Shey Gompa
- Day 12: Shey Gompa to Namgung Gompa / 6-7 hrs crossing Shey La Pass (5100m)
Today we will head to our destination Namgung Gompa. You will witness barren lands and also the area where the Himalayan Caravan movie was covered.
With exploration around, we will head towards the She La(ridge). From the top, you will have view of Kanjiroba, Mustang, Tiber, and many mountains around.
After a few more walk ahead we will reach Namgung Gompa.
Overnight at Namgung Gompa.
- Day 13: Namgong Gompa to Saldang/ 4-5 hrs camping.
It is the day to witness drylands with different vegetation of Dolpo. Our Trek will be 5-6 hours long, We will start our trail with normal descend enjoying the landscape.
Overnight at Saldang.
- Day 14: Saldang to Day hike to Yangjer Gumba/ 4599m
Today we will have an acclimatization day at Sadang. This night we will not change our camp. This day is also taken as Trek high and sleep low. It is kept in order to keep you safe from the altitude sickness.
Today we will hike to Yanjer Gompa which is a breathtaking location. After enjoying the beautiful location we will hike back to Saldang for overnight.
Overnight at Saldang.
- Day 15: Saldang to Khoma village
As we finish our breakfast we will head towards Khoma Village. As earlier, we will be crossing small hill ridges to reach the next camp. Today we will pas Khom La (la= ridge) The ridge is 4500m after passing the ridge we will arrive at Khoma with normal descend.
Overnight at Khoma Village
- Day 16: Khoma village to Shimen
These trails are very less crowded. If you are lucky enough then you might encounter some such as Blue Sheep. And again we will face another ridge known as Shimen La (4260m). After enjoying the beautiful view from the ridge we will move forward and descend down and reach Shimen.
Overnight at Shimen.
- Day 17: Shimen to Tinje
As we finish your breakfast we will head to Tinje. Tinje is a small village between hills. Our trails will be together side of a river called Panjyang Khola.
You will have sight of some small villages and monasteries. After continuous trek for 5-6 hours we will reach Tinje.
Overnight at Tinje.
- Day 18: Tinje to Yak Kharka
As usual, we will head after having our breakfast. Today our Trek will be 5-6 hours long. we will pass through dry lands and small ridges.
Our today’s destination is Yak Kharka. Kharka means a grazing ground.
Overnight at Yak Kharka.
- Day 19: Yak Kharka (4780m) to Chharka / 5 hrs walk crossing Mola Pass (5030m)
Today we will make another pass and head to Charka. The pass we cross today is Mola Pass which has an elevation of 5030m.
Our Trek will be 6-7 hours long.
Overnight at Charka.
- Day 20: Chharka to Norbulung/ Nulungsumda
We will have our breakfast and start making our Trek over highlands. You will witness beautiful hills and landscape.
After a continuous walk to 6-7 hours, you will reach Norbulung.
Overnight ar Norbulung.
- Day 21: Nulungsumda/ Norbulung to Ghalden Ghuldum (4247m) Crossing Niwala (5120m) and Jungbenla (5550m) passes.
Today will be a tough day. We will have a good breakfast, pack some chocolates for high passes. Our task of the day is to pass two high passes Niwa La Pass and Jungbenla Pass.
You will have an amazing sight of nearby hills and villages. After crossing over both the asses and short hike you will arrive at Ghaldang Ghuldung which gives a sense of bumpy place.
Overnight at Ghaldang Ghuldung
- Day 22: Ghalden Ghuldum/ (4247m) to Sangta/ (3777m)
Today our overnight stay will be at an isolated village from where you can see the beautiful views of hills around.
Today our trail is more descends with some ascends at different points.
Overnight at Sangta.
- Day 23: Santa to Phalyak/ (3175m)
As we finish our breakfast we will head towards the hill of Santa. Today we will pass across many small ridges.
After we cross 8-9 small ridges at distant and then we will descend down towards Kali Gandaki Valley.
After 6-7 hours of continuous walk you will reach to Phalyak.
Overnight at Phalyak.
- Day 24: Phalyak to Jomsom/ (2720m)
Today we will head to Jomsom. Our trails are comparatively short but it will be quite challenging. Our trails will be rocky.
Today your Trek will be 5-6 hours long Trek.
Overnight at Jomsom
- Day 25: Flight from Jomsom to Pokhara
As we finish our Breakfast today we will have direct flight from Jomsom to Pokhara.
Our flight will be early in the morning so, if you can freshen yourself as you reach pokhara and explore around.
Overnight at Pokhara.
- Day 26: Flight from Pokhara to Kathmandu
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped to Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Upper Dolpa with Jomsom Trek, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
On timely arrival to Kathmandu, you can use the free time to explore and shop for your family, or else you can also make a short city tour.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 27: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone and a certificate for your completion of Upper Dolpa with Jomsom Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
