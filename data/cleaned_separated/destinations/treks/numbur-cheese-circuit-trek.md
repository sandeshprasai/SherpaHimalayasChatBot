Discover the Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek: A Hidden Gem in the Himalayas
Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek is a recently opened non-tourist area trekking route in the lap of Numbur Chuli. The ideal trek, for who wants to escape from the regular tracks and for who wants to touch up with the off beaten trails.This recently developed trekking route lies Ramechhap about 190 km east of the capital city of Kathmandu. The trek leads through deep river gorges, glacial lakes, terraced rice fields, subtropical forests and Sherpa villages to the Likhu Khola glacier, watched over by the towering peaks of Pathibarra and Numburchuli.
This trek provides breathtaking views of the majestic Himalayan peaks including Numburchuli (6959m), Sarmoche (5255m), Ramdung (5930m), Chukyima Go (6959), Parchamo (6273m), Bigphrea Go Nup (6666m), Paniyo Tippa (6696m), Paniyo Shar (6549m), Everest range, Annapurna South, Langtang, Jugal Himal and Gaurishankar.
Beginning the Journey
The journey commences with a scenic drive from Kathmandu to Shivalaya, a small town perched on the edge of the Dudh Koshi River. From here, the trekking adventure unfolds as you traverse through dense rhododendron and pine forests, accompanied by the melodic sounds of native birds and occasional glimpses of wildlife. The trail gradually ascends, offering stunning vistas of terraced fields and traditional Sherpa houses adorned with prayer flags, a testament to the region’s rich Buddhist heritage.
Cultural Encounters and Local Hospitality
One of the highlights of the Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek is the opportunity to immerse yourself in Sherpa culture and hospitality. Along the way, you’ll encounter friendly locals going about their daily lives, tending to their fields and livestock. Visits to ancient monasteries and stupas provide insight into the spiritual practices that have shaped the lives of Sherpa communities for generations. A visit to the Numbur Cheese Factory offers a unique cultural experience where you can witness traditional cheese-making techniques and sample freshly made Himalayan cheese.
Scenic Landscapes and High-Altitude Highlights
As the trek progresses, the landscape transitions from lush green valleys to rugged alpine terrain dotted with yak pastures and pristine lakes. A highlight for many trekkers is reaching the sacred Panch Pokhari (Five Lakes), a site revered by both Buddhist and Hindu pilgrims. Here, at an altitude of over 4,000 meters, you can marvel at the crystal-clear waters reflecting the surrounding snow-capped peaks, including Numbur Himal and Khatang.
Challenging Passes and Spectacular Views
The Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek also offers adventurous souls the opportunity to conquer high mountain passes such as the Shivalaya Pass and the Khola Kharka Pass, each offering breathtaking panoramic views of the Himalayan range. These challenging but rewarding sections of the trek provide trekkers with a sense of accomplishment and a chance to capture stunning photographs of the rugged landscape.
Accommodation and Cuisine
Accommodation along the Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek ranges from cozy teahouses in the lower valleys to basic lodges in higher-altitude regions. Despite the remoteness of the trekking route, you can expect warm hospitality and hearty meals prepared with locally sourced ingredients. Traditional Nepali dishes such as dal bhat (rice and lentils) and Tibetan bread are staples that provide much-needed energy for the trek ahead.
Conclusion
In conclusion, the Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek offers a truly immersive Himalayan experience that combines natural beauty, cultural heritage, and adventure in equal measure. Whether you’re a seasoned trekker or embarking on your first trekking adventure, this hidden gem of Nepal promises unforgettable memories and a deeper appreciation for the majesty of the Himalayas. Join us on the Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek and discover why this lesser-known trekking route captivates the hearts of all who venture into its pristine wilderness.

Check this The best time to travel the Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek in Nepal is during two seasons: 1. Spring (March to May) 2. Autumn (September to November) Both seasons offer optimal trekking conditions, allowing you to fully enjoy the natural beauty and cultural experiences along the Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek. It's important to note that while these are peak seasons, the trek is less crowded compared to more popular routes like the Everest Base Camp or Annapurna Circuit, offering a more serene and authentic Himalayan experience. Avoid trekking during the monsoon season (June to August) due to heavy rainfall, which can lead to slippery trails, leeches, and limited visibility of the mountains. Similarly, winter (December to February) brings cold temperatures and snow, making higher altitude trekking challenging and potentially hazardous. By planning your trek during the spring or autumn months, you'll maximize your chances of enjoying favorable weather, stunning views, and a memorable adventure on the Numbur Cheese Circuit Trek.
