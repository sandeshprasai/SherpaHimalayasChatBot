Helambu Gosaikunda Trek is an excellent way of exploring the Himalayan Ranges within a week. Gosainkunda is a holy place, especially for Hindus and Buddhists. A grand Holy festival is held in Gosainkunda Lake during the month of August. A legendary fact about this place is that it is covered with 108 lakes, most of which are visible during the journey.
Exhilarating trails, campsites with wonderful outlooks, culturally and religiously rich village settlements, a high pass, and the sacred visit to the holy Gosainkunda Lake are the things that are to be expected from this trek. Along the journey, we can explore the legendary Yolmo culture of villages in the high mountains, get marvel vistas of lonely high mountains and watch the farmers toil in the fields using handmade tools. The culturally abundant villages of the Helambu Gosainkunda trekking are amiable, warm, and very welcoming.
The Helambu Gosaikunda Trek trail will lead us across splendid frozen lakes, beautifully woven Sherpa hamlets, vibrant Rhododendron forests, various Shrubs, exotic Orchids, Wildlife inhabitants, and breathtaking sights of the snow-clad Himalayas. We will also get an opportunity to view the magnificent Mt. Dhaulagiri which stretches all the way to Mt. Everest and the alpine yak pasture of Kyanjin Gompa.
The Helambu Gosainkunda trekking trek starts at North of Kathmandu Sundarijal village and finished at Dhunche village in the Langtang area. From there take the bus back to Kathmandu. The forests that fall along  are well known for their Wildlife inhabitants like the Red Pandas, Himalayan Black Bear, Wild Boar, and Langur Monkeys. The views of snow-capped mountains-Langtang Lirung, Pemthang Ri, Langshisha Ri, Dorje Lakpa, and Kangchenpo, Tilman’s fluted peak is a must experience.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival in kathmandu
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport of Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be Taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 02: Drive to sundarijal and Trek to Chisapani (2165m)
Today we will drive 30 mins away from Kathmandu to nearby countryside; Sundarijal. Sundarijal is the gateway of Shivapuri National Park.
As we reach sundarijal we will start our hike towards Chisapani. The rail will be all through the dense forest of Shivapuri. You trek will be 4-5 hours long.
Chisapani is a beautiful village from where you will see beautiful view of surrounding mountains such as Mt. Ganesh and Mt. Dorje Lhakpa etc
Overnight at Chisapani.
- Day 03: Trek To Kutumsang (2470m )
We will head towards Kutumsang as we finish our breakfast. Today will be a long day trek. Our trek will normally be 6-7 Hours long.
Today your trails will be filled with Langtang Range Mountains, Meadows, and Fields. These are the main attraction of today’s Trail
Overnight at Kutumsang
- Day 04: Trek to ThadePati (3690m)
After a beautiful exploration of Kutumsang we will head towards Thadepati. From the ridge of todays trail you can see breathtaking view Gosaikunda Peak and Langtang Mountain.
You will also come across rhododendron forest. We will reach Thadepati after continuous walk of around 6 hours.
Overnight at Thadepati.
- Day 05: Trek to Phedi (3730m)
Today as we have our breakfast we will climb towards Phedi. Phedi is a small village above Helambu village at an elevation of 3730m.
This is also the camp before one of the most adventurous and amazing pass.
Overnight at Phedi
- Day 06 : Trek to Gosaikunda (4380m) Via Laurebina Pass (4610m)
Today is the pass day where we will pass over one of the beautiful pass called Laurebina Pass.
From the top of the pass we can see breathtaking view of Langtang range Himalayas.
As we come across pass we will trek for some hour long and reach Gosaikunda Lake.
Overnight at Gosaikunda
- Day 07: Trek to ChandanBari / Sing Gompa(3330m) and then to Dhunche (1960m)
Today we will descend down towards Sing Gompa. Sing Gompa is a small village in the upper hill side of Langtang region. The tea houses are small and foods here found are more local. We will have our Lunch and trek towards Dhunche. Today your trek will be 6-7 hours of Trek.
Dhunche is a small town. Here we will be our Overnight stay. The next day we will take vehicle from here and drive back to Kathmandu
Overnight at Dhunche
- Day 08: Drive from Dhunche to Kathmandu
Today we will drive back to Kathmandu. Your drive will be around 5 hours. We will reach Kathmandu by afternoon.
As we reach Kathmandu you can freshen up yourself and explore Kathmandu in the evening.
- Day 09: Free Day in Kathmandu
Today will be a freeday. You can explore around and make your shopping if needed.
Today you can explore the UNESCO Heritage sites around which are within very short range.
UNESCO Sites nearby:
- Swyambhunath Stup
- Boudhanath Stupa
- Kathmandu Durbar square
- Pashupatinath Temple
- Patan Durbar Square
- Bhaktapur Durbar Square
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 10: Final Departure
Today morning you Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for last meet. You will be dropped at airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life and Harmony to everyone and certificate for your completion of Helambu Gosaikunda Lake Trek with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave.
