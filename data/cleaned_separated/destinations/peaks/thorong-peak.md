Thorong peak (6,144 m) climbing is one of the rewarding and technically difficult peaks climbing in the Annapurna region of Nepal. Thorong peak is a beautiful snow peak standing to the south of the Thorung La in Annapurna region. This peak climbing is the excellent combination of the classic Annapurna round trek passing through the high pass of Thorong La Pass and rewarding Thorong peak climbing. Thorung La peak climbing was opened recently for the adventurer’s affecter Nepal government derestricted the trek to Manang valley.
Thorong peak (6,144 m) climbing starts after we start trekking from the lowland valley of Bensi Sahar. Our trekking trail takes us through the Marsyangdi River, lowland farms, villages, forests, falls, hills and dales offering a diverse range of flora and fauna and the most spectacular mountain scenery of Annapurna and Manaslu to reach Tetang. The region is mostly inhabited by Gurung people practicing typical culture and traditions. Here you get an opportunity to experience the rich culture of these people and climb through the most demanding pass in the region. From Tetang the trekking trail descends via Gya La at (3,970 m.) to join the main route from Manang to Ranipauwa. Now passing the Thorung La pass we head up to the summit of the peak. The summit of this spectacular peak offers the most magnificent mountain view of the Nilgiri peak, Tilicho peak, Dhaulagiri range, Annapurna range along with the beautiful scenery of the Mustang region and beautiful Kali Gandaki valley. You can’t get the information of the region in the maps of Nepal.

Day 01: Arrival day in Kathmandu (1300m)
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trek Preparation Day
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Thorang Peak Climb. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase it from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and the rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Drive from Kathmandu to Besi Sahar and drive to Dharapani (1960m)
Today we will have a long day drive. As we finish our breakfast we will head towards Dharapani via Besisahar. It will take 6 hours to drive to Besi Sahar by private vehicle. After short rest, we will again have to drive 2 hours long offroad to reach afternoon camp Dharapani.
This drive will be very interesting and the perfect time to know more about the landscape of Nepal.
Overnight at Dharapani
- Day 05: Trek from Dharapani to Chame (2,710m/8,891feet), 5 - 6 hours
Today is our first Trek day. We will start our Trek from Dharapani, heading towards Chame which is elevated at an altitude of 2710m.
Your trek will be 5-6 hours long along the riverside enjoying beautiful nature and mountain such as Mt. Lamjung and Mt. Annapurna
Overnight at Chame
- Day 06: Trek from Chame to Pisang (3,300m/10824feet), 5 - 6 hours
After we finish our breakfast we will head towards Pisang. The trek will be normal with slight slopes. It will take Normally 5 hours to reach Pisang.
You will find two Pisang; Upper Pisang and Lower Pisang. The camp site will be decided by then while we get close to. Both the Pisang are nearby so, it is similar wherever we stay for camp. You can witness Mt. Annapurna II directly from Pisang
Overnight at Pisang
- Day 07: Pisang to Manang (3,500m/11,482feet), 6 - 7 hours
On the day 5th the trails are almost similar to the past day. Today we will reach a beautiful small village called Manang. Manang is also the perfect location to acclimatize your body with the altitude.
With normal 6-7 hours of Trek, we will reach Manang.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 08: Manang: Acclimatization day
Today is the acclimatization day. This day is specially kept to let your body acclimatize with the altitude and the environment. You can take this day as a rest day too.
This day is also known as the hike up sleep low. Today we will hike to Chongur View Point crossing through Gangapurna Glacier. you will also encounter a beautiful glacier lake.
After we finish our exploration around we will come back to camp and be prepared for the next day.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 09: Manang to Yak Kharka (4,110m/13,484feet): 3 - 4 hours
After a day’s rest, we will now again our Trek routine as usual. We will head ahead after having our breakfast. Today our destination is Yak Kharka.
Yak Kharka is a small village located between two big hills. Today we will have a short Trek which usually takes 4 hours. We will be there at yak Kharka for lunch and explore around.
Overnight at Yak kharka
- Day 11: Trek from Yak Kharka to horang High Camp(4800m/15748 ft) 5-6 hours
Today your Trek will be longer than the past day. We are now closed to Thorongla Pass. Our today’s camp will be below the pass; which is also known as thoroughly pass.
It will normally Take 6-7 hours, but the trail is not much hard. We will hike normally with gentle slopes and with normal uphills.
Overnight at Thornag High Camp
- Day 10: Trek from Thorang High Camp to Thorang La/ Thorang Base Camp (5416m)
Today is the day to reach up to your main destination of this Annapurna Circuit Trek. It dreams of millions but only a few take this encouraging trek and make their life filled with beautiful memories and adventure.
We will trek as we finish our breakfast. We will trek uphill until we reach Thorongla Pass (5416m). The trail is not much difficult.
After a continuous 3-4 hours climb of uphill slopes, we will reach Thorongla Pass. which is also the main attraction of Annapurna Circuit Trek. Your Camp will be set close to Thorongla which will also be the base camp of Thorang Peak
This point can let you view two big and famous trekking regions of Nepal. Annapurna Region and Mustang region.
Oovernight at Thorang la
- Day 11: Summit to Thorong peak (6100m) and climb down to Thorong la
Today will be the total climbing day. You will have to be prepared the previous day with all the gears and you start your climb at night time. Usually, your climb will be from 2-3 am. It is because as the sunrise there may be a risk of small avalanche, loosen of rocks due to melting of ice, and with a bright sun in the snow, it will definitely be a hard climb. The wind is also the major factor that you climb early in the morning. In the morning there will be very wind which makes it a lot easy and safe.
From the summit, you will have a breathtaking view of many mountains which includes Manaslu(8163m), Annapurna I (8091 m), Manaslu East (7992m), Himal Chuli (7893m), Annapurna IV (7525m), Gangapurna (7455m), Tarke Kang (7193m), and many more peaks
After a memorable time at the summit, you climb down to base camp.
Overnight at Thorongla
- Day 12: Trek from Thorongla to Muktinath (3800m)
Today you will have a long and challenging descend towards Muktinath. Your descend will be 3-4 hours long. Today your destination will be Muktinath; a holy pilgrimage place for both Hindu and Buddhist people.
Overnight at Muktinath
- Day 13: Trek from Muktinath to Jomsom (2500m), 5-6 hours
Today our camp will be Jomsom. We will either trek or drive to Jomsom.
It will normally take 5-6 hours by trek and 1 hour by drive. If you decide to trek down then you will visit one of the most historic villages in mustang Village known as Kagbeni.
You will witness small mud houses and old temples with beautiful Tibetan lifestyle. your trek will be a worth decision going through Kagbeni.
Overnight at Jomsom
- Day 14: Flight from Jomsom to Pokhara (8 20m), 20 min.
Today you will have a 30 min morning flight from Jomsom to Pokhara early in the morning. Jomsom gets hit by strong wind after 12 pm hence afternoon flight is not possible.
As you reach Pokhara in the morning before 10 am you use this day to explore the beautiful lakeside city. You can get adventure activities such as Bunjee and Paragliding in Pokhara. If you mention Sherpa Himalaya Team about the need for adventure activity then it would be appreciated and time will be managed accordingly.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 15: Drive / Flight from Pokhara to Kathmandu
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by the Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped at Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Thorang Peak Climb, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 16: Free Day in Kathmandu
Today will be a free day. Today you can prepare for your departure and also make a short city tour around and also use the day for shopping for some gifts for your family.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 17: Final Departure
Today morning your Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone, and a certificate for your completion of Thorong Peak with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
