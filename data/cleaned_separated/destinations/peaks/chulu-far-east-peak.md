Chulu Far East Peak Climbing is one of the most exhilarating mountaineering programs in Nepal’s Annapurna region. This peak at 6,059 meters stands ideal for ventures that seek both trekking and alpine climbing without the involvement of advanced technical skills. The journey begins with a trek through the beautiful Annapurna Circuit, passing by villages such as Manang, which allows insight into the culture. Participants will also cross-cut across various landscapes from the terraces to high-altitude deserts as the gradual climb-up ensues, offering spectacular views of Annapurna II, III, IV, and Gangapurna. The basic trekking skills required for this climb involve ice axes, crampons, and ropes. Therefore, it is suitable for trekkers with average experience. It also covers crossing the Thorong La Pass at 5,416 meters, an added adventure to the already fantastic experience. Chulu Far East Peak climbing is ideal for those in search of the thrill of climbing one of the peaks of the Himalayas amidst the rich culture and stunning landscapes of Nepal.

Itinerary
-
Day 01: Arrival day in Kathmandu (1300m)
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Trek preparation Day
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide for Chulu Far East Peak Climb. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase it from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and the rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.
We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Drive Kathmandu to Besi sahar and same day drive to Dharapani (1860m), 6-8 hours
Today we will have a long day drive. As we finish our breakfast we will head towards Dharapani via Besisahar. It will take 6 hours to drive to Besi Sahar by private vehicle. After short rest, we will again have to drive 2 hours long offroad to reach afternoon camp Dharapani.
This drive will be very interesting and the perfect time to know more about the landscape of Nepal.
Overnight at Dharapani
- Day 04: Trek from Dharapani to Chame (2713m), 4-5 hours
Today is our first Trek day. We will start our Trek from Dharapani, heading towards Chame which is elevated at an altitude of 2710m.
Your trek will be 5-6 hours long along the riverside enjoying beautiful nature and mountain such as Mt. Lamjung and Mt. Annapurna
Overnight at Chame
- Day 05: Trek Chame to Lower Pisang (3150m), 4-6 hours
After we finish our breakfast we will head towards Pisang. The trek will be normal with slight slopes. It will take Normally 5 hours to reach Pisang.
You will find two Pisang; Upper Pisang and Lower Pisang. Your campsite will be Lower Pisang. You can witness Mt. Annapurna II directly from Pisang
Overnight at Lower Pisang
- Day 06: Trek from Lower Pisang to Ngawal (3660m)
Today your destination will be Ngawal. Ngawal is a small village with Manang community people. Your trails start with a normal uphill towards a village called Ghyaru, where you will encounter some small and very old Buddhist monasteries.
As you go further towards Ngawal you will pass through the Mani wall; walls made with Tibetan prayers and arts on stones. Today your walk will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Ngawal
- Day 07: Trek from Ngawal to Base Camp (3,910m), 5-6 hours
A tough day trek day. Today as usual your trek starts after you finish your breakfast. You will pass through small monasteries and mani’s. Until you reach a small village called Julu your trails are normal slopes. You will face steep uphill climb up to Base Camp after Julu. Today your climb will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Base Camp.
- Day 08: Climb from Base Camp to High camp (4,955m) 4-5 hours
Today your first-day climb towards Chulu Far East starts. Your trails are mostly rocky and snowy where crampons will be necessary. You will have a beautiful view of Mt.Pisang, Mt. Chulu West, Far East, Annapurna Range Himalayas, and many small peaks around.
After 4-5 hours of continuous climb, you will arrive at High Camp, where our Sherpa will find the camping spot and fix the camp.
Overnight at High Camp
- Day 09: Summit day(Chulu Far East 6059m)- back to Base Camp
Today will be the total climbing day. You will have to be prepared the previous day with all the gears and you start your climb at night time. Usually, your climb will be from 2-3 am. It is because as the sunrise there may be a risk of small avalanche, loosen of rocks due to melting of ice, and with a bright sun in the snow, it will definitely be a hard climb. The wind is also the major factor that you climb early in the morning. In the morning there will be very wind which makes it a lot easy and safe.
From the summit, you will have a panoramic view of many small peaks including two 8000m + mountains. Mt.Manaslu 8163m, Mt.Annapurna I-IV range, Mt.Himal Chuli 7893m, Mt.Tarke Kang 7193m, Mt.Lamjung Himal 6931m, Mt.Chombi 6704m, Mt.Chulu East 6439m, Mt.Saribung 6328m, and many peaks.
Overnight at Base Camp
- Day 10: Trek Base Camp to Manang (3,500)
Today after an adventurous climb of Chulu Far East 6095 m, you descend towards Manag Village. Today you will again be introduced to the Annapurna Circuit Trail. From this day you will have another adventurous trek of Annapurna Circuit trek. Today your walk will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Manang
- Day 11: Trek to Yak Kharka / Churi ledhar (4,200m)
You will now again trek with the usual routine. We will head ahead after having our breakfast. Today our destination is Yak Kharka.
Yak Kharka is a small village located between two big hills. Today we will have a short Trek which usually takes 4 hours. We will be there at yak Kharka for lunch and explore around.
Overnight at Yak kharka
- Day 12: Trek to Thorang High Camp (4,800)
Today your Trek will be longer than the past day. We are now closed to Thorongla Pass. Our today’s camp will be below the pass; which is also known as thorong la pass.
It will normally Take 5-6 hours, but the trail is not much hard. We will hike normally with gentle slopes and with normal uphills.
Overnight at Thorang High Camp
- Day 13: Trek to Thorang-La pass(5,416m) and then to Muktinath (3,800m)
today is the day to reach up to your final & main destination of this entire Trek. It dreams of millions but only a few take this encouraging trek and make their life filled with beautiful memories and adventure.
We will trek as we finish our breakfast. We will trek uphill until we reach Thorongla Pass (5416m). The trail is not much difficult.
After a continuous 3-4 hours climb of uphill slopes, we will reach Thorongla Pass. which is also the main attraction of Annapurna Circuit Trek. We will enjoy our successful Trek there and take some beautiful pictures capturing the memories.
This point can let you view two big and famous trekking regions of Nepal. Annapurna Region and Mustang region.
Now after the pass, we will enter into mustang region. Today our stay will be at Muktinath (3800m). It will take normally 2 hours downhill.
Overnight at Muktinath
- Day 14: Trek from Muktinath to Jomsom (2,800m)
Today our camp will be Jomsom. We will either trek or drive to Jomsom.
It will normally take 5-6 hours by trek and 1 hour by drive. If you decide to trek down then you will visit one of the most historic villages in mustang Village known as Kagbeni.
You will witness small mud houses and old temples with beautiful Tibetan lifestyle. your trek will be a worth decision going through Kagbeni.
Overnight at Jomsom
- Day 15: Jomsom To Pokhara by Flight
Today you will have a 30 min morning flight from Jomsom to Pokhara early in the morning. Jomsom gets hit by strong wind after 12 pm hence afternoon flight is not possible.
As you reach Pokhara in the morning before 10 am you use this day to explore the beautiful lakeside city. You can get adventure activities such as Bunjee and Paragliding in Pokhara. If you mention Sherpa Himalaya Team about the need for adventure activity then it would be appreciated and time will be managed accordingly.
Overnight at Pokhara
- Day 16: Pokhara to Kathmandu Drive by Jeep/ car
Today we will fly / Drive from Pokhara to Kathmandu as planned with Company. You will be picked by the Sherpa Himalaya representative and dropped at Hotel.
After this beautiful and adventurous Chulu Far East Peak Climb, you can have a proper rest at your hotel.
- Day 17: free day in Kathmandu
Today will be a free day. Today you can prepare for your departure and also make a short city tour around and also use the day for shopping for some gifts for your family.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 18: Final Departure
Today morning your Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone, and a certificate for your completion of Chulu Far East Peak with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
Thank You for your co-operation with Sherpa Himalaya
