Chulu Far East Peak Climbing is one of the most exhilarating mountaineering programs in Nepal’s Annapurna...
Paldor peak (5,896m ) lies at the south-east end of Ganesh Himal range at the junction of the Tiru and Karpu Dandas (ridges). While it is under 6,000m, it provides a satisfying and non-to-difficult climb. The main route across the glacier is crevassed and takes some finding. On reaching the col there are two options. Either climb the straightforward ridge using ropes, or continue around to the crevassed back for a simpler, 45 degree, ridge. In good conditions making a traverse of the mountain is possible.
From the summit there are excellent views of the Ganesh Himal peaks, Langtang Lirung (7,227 m) and Manaslu (8,156 m) as well as the mountains of Tibet including Shishapangma (8,013 m).

Day 01: Arrival in Kathmandu Airport
On this day you will be landed at the Capital City of Nepal-Kathmandu (1300m/4265 ft). It is the only International Airport in Nepal. You will be received by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Then with a brief introduction, you will be welcomed by flower Garland. You will be taken to your Hotel where you can rest and freshen up to keep away Jet Lag. You will get a nearby location map from the hotel reception and you can explore around.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 02: Preparation Day in Kathmandu
Today you will also be introduced to your respective Guide and Climber for Paldor Peak. If you have anything to buy for your trek then you can purchase it from the stores around. In the evening you will have to pack only the necessary and important items that you need during the Trek and the rest you can either store at Hotel or with Sherpa Himalaya.We recommend you use the company Duffel bag as there will be less chance of misplacing in an airport and easier to find in case of missing.
Overnight at KTM Hotel
- Day 03: Drive to Sybrubesi (1470m) by private Jeep
Today will be a long day’s drive. We will be having drive mostly in the Hilly Region of Nepal. This Drive can provide you the detailed landscape of the hilly region.
We will head as towards Syabrubesi from Kathmandu as we finish our breakfast. It will be 6-7 hours of drive.
We will reach Syabrubesi by evening and you can explore the small town.
Overnight at Syabrubesi
- Day 04: Trek from Sybrubesi to Gompa Gaon ( 2238m) 5-6 hour
Today your morning Trek starts as you finish your breakfast. Today will be a long day trek towards Gompa Gaon. It is a small village inhabitant by Tamang Community people and you will find small tea houses and monasteries. Your trek will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Gompa Gaon.
- Day 05: Trek from Gompa Gaon - Somdang (3220m)
Today will be a challenging day. You will be introduced with more uphill than previous days. Today you will have to cross a small pass of 3000m high known as Khurpu Danda(ridge) pass. After the pass, your trails get easier with a normal slope. After crossing a river called Mailing Khola you will reach Somdang.
- Day 06: Trek from Somdang - Jastha Khani (3700m)
As usual, as you finish your breakfast, today your destination will be Jascha Khani. Jastha Khani is a small village where you will get a perfect opportunity to know the culture and tradition of the Tamang community people. Today your trek will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Jastha Khani
- Day 07: Jastha Khani- Base Camp Paldor B.C (4980)
Your trails are mostly uphill from Jastha Khani. With a continuous climb of 5-6 hours, you will reach Paldor Base Camp. You will have view of Mountains like Mt. Ganesh 7118m, Mt. Pabil 7104m, and some small peaks
Overnight at Base Camp
- Day 08: Climb from Base Camp to High Camp ( 4980 m)
Today your climb starts towards Paldor High Camp. You will need all your gears prepared. Crampons will be necessary today. Your trail is an uphill climb through rocks and snow. Your climb will be 4-5 hours long.
Your camp will be set by the Sherpa team and today you rest here for acclimatization and prepare yourself for the next early morning climb to the summit.
Overnight at High Camp
- Day 10: Summit Paldor (5896 m) & Return to Base Camp
Today will be the total climbing day. You will have to be prepared the previous day with all the gears and you start your climb at night time. Usually, your climb will be from 2-3 am. It is because as the sunrise there may be a risk of small avalanche, loosen of rocks due to melting of ice, and with a bright sun in the snow, it will definitely be a hard climb. The wind is also the major factor that you climb early in the morning. In the morning there will be very wind which makes it a lot easy and safe.
From the summit, you will have a breathtaking view of more than 20 well-known mountains. Mt. Manaslu 8163m, Mt. Shishapangma 8027m, Mt. Annuprna ii 7937m, Mt. Himal Chuli 7893m, Mt. Ngadi Chuli 7871m, Mt. Annapurna IV-7525m, Mt. Langtang Lirung 7227m, Mt. Gauri Shankar 7134m, Mt. Ganesh 7118m, Mt. Numbur 6959m, and many countless peaks.
After your beautiful time at the summit, you will return back to Base camp.
Overnight at Base Camp
- Day 12: Trek back from Base Camp to Somdang Kharka
Celebrating your success of Paldor Peak Climb, you will now descend back. Your descending trail is the same by which you climbed towards base camp, Today your trek will be 5-6 hours long. You will reach Somdang Kharka at around 2-3 pm.
Overnight at Somdang Kharka
- Day 13: Trek from Somdang Kharka to Gatlang
A day to again into the crowds of villagers. After a week you will be involved with villagers and more human settlement. At Gatlang you can have a bath and freshen up yourself after this adventurous climb of Paldor Peak. Your returning trail will be the same which you used earlier. Your trek will be 5-6 hours long.
Overnight at Gatlang
- Day 14 : Trek from Gatlang to Sybrubesi
Today you will reach the main town of the Langtang region. You will have good quality hotels here. Your trip will be flavored with a natural hot spring close to the river where you can take your tiredness and stress away by having a bath in a hot spring. Today will also be your last day with all staff members. We will greet each other for making such a fantastic climb and have last dinner together
Overnight at Syabru Besi
- Day 15: Drive to Kathmandu by Private Jeep
As we finish our breakfast today we will drive back to Kathmandu. The returning path is same which we came earlier.
We will reach Kathmandu by evening.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 16: Free Day in Kathmandu
Today will be a free day. Today you can prepare for your departure and also make a short city tour around and also use the day for shopping for some gifts for your family.
Overnight at Kathmandu
- Day 17: Final Departure
Today morning your Sherpa Himalaya representative will be there at your hotel for the last meet. You will be dropped at the airport considering airport time.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-tak) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone, and a certificate for your completion of Paldor Peak Climb with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste, and a hand wave.
