Annapurna Base Camp Helicopter Tour is one hour round trip from Pokhara. Mount Annapurna and its ranges are very near at Annapurna Base Camp. 15 minutes fly with the helicopter through panoramic Mountain and scenic landscape reach ABC. Beautiful Mount Fishtail, Mount Annapurna I (8,091m), Mount Dhaulagiri (8,167m) and other Himalayan rages are views as 360 degrees. Stay half an hour in ABC (4,130 miters) and explore around. The helicopter is able to carry 4 to 5 peoples depend on total weight.
This tour will be of total 4 days in Nepal. As you arrive here in Kathmandu Sherpa Himalaya representative will pick you from TIA Kathmandu airport and will drop at the hotel. Our representative will explain you about the tour and also the city. If your arrival times you in the morning then it can be perfect opportunity to explore some of the UNESCO world heritage site.
The Next day (Day 02), you can either have a flight or drive to Pokhara. The drive will take normally 5-6 hours and can extend depending upon the traffic. If you choose to take a flight then it will take only 20-25 minutes which will give you proper time to explore the beautiful Pokhara city.
Pokhara is considered as one of the most beautiful city of Nepal as you can find mountains and hills on its surrounding with a lake. Your stay will be at lakeside. You can explore and experience boating, paragliding and even bungee if your time allows. If you would like to make extra adventure then proper communication with Sherpa Himalaya team would be best so that proper time management can be done.
After the stay at Pokhara on the 4th Day we will have early morning Helicopter flight to Pokhara. You can witness amazing panoramic view of whole Annapurna Range mountains including Langtang regions Himalayas too. After breathtaking flight you will be dropped at Annapurna Base Camp elevated at an altitude of 13545 ft / 4130m.
You will have 10-15 minute landing time there at the base camp. You can capture beautiful pictures and great Mt. Annapurna. After your precious time at the base camp you will flight back to Pokhara and after having quick lunch you will be departed to Kathmandu.
If you have one more days then you can add a extra day to explore Kathmandu City full of UNESCO World Heritage Sites. If time limits then according the program set with Sherpa Himalaya and flight scheduled you will be dropped to TIA airport by Sherpa Himalaya Representative.
Sherpa Himalaya will thank you for an amazing time together and handover Buddhist Scarf (Kha-ta) which symbolizes peace, prosperity, Long life, and Harmony to everyone and a certificate for your completion of Annapurna Heli Tour with Sherpa Himalaya.
With a hope to see all the beautiful people again, a warm Namaste and a hand wave
